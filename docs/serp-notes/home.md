## What the top results cover

Directories (Finelib, GoAfrica, HeliumDoc, ConnectCiti), many job adverts for dermatologists, news items about the LUTH dermatology centre, profiles of individual consultants, and blog posts listing "top dermatologists in Lagos". Very few results are a clinic's own page with real depth.

## Format that wins

Lists and directory entries win by default. A first-party clinic page that states who the doctor is, what is treated, the address and the hours has little competition for depth.
