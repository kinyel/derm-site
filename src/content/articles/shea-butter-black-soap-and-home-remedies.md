## How should I judge a home remedy?

Ask three questions. Is there any evidence that it works? What is the worst that can happen if it does not? And is it delaying a treatment that would work? Skin with more melanin answers irritation with dark marks, so a remedy that merely "stings a bit" on light skin can leave months of discolouration on brown and Black skin.

## Which remedies help?

**Shea butter (ori).** Rich in fatty acids, it softens and seals in water. Suitable for dry body skin, cracked heels, lips and harmattan dryness. Choose unrefined, unscented shea butter from a clean source; apply to damp skin. Limits: too heavy for acne-prone faces and for babies in hot weather, where it can cause heat rash. Rarely, people with latex allergy react to it.

**Petroleum jelly.** A reliable barrier for chapped lips, cracked heels, nappy areas and healing wounds. It does not add moisture; it keeps it in. Avoid on acne-prone facial skin.

**Coconut oil.** Useful on the body and as a pre-wash hair treatment. Not for acne-prone faces.

**Sunflower seed oil.** One of the few plant oils shown to support the skin barrier.

**Aloe vera gel.** Soothing on mild sunburn and irritation. Use plain gel; a few people are allergic.

**Oatmeal baths (colloidal oatmeal).** Calm itchy, dry skin.

**Salt-water soaks** for minor infected toenails and mouth ulcers.

**Cool compresses** for itch, bites and swelling.

## Which are neutral or unproven?

**African black soap (ose dudu).** Made from plantain skin or cocoa pod ash and oils. It is a cleanser, nothing more. Because it is alkaline, it can strip the skin's protective acid layer. If you like it, dilute it, use it on the body, follow with moisturiser, and avoid versions with added "whitening" ingredients.

**Turmeric.** Has anti-inflammatory compounds in laboratory studies, with limited evidence on skin. It stains, and commercial turmeric soaps and creams sometimes contain undeclared lightening agents.

**Honey, cucumber, milk and yoghurt masks.** Pleasant, low risk, little effect.

**Rice water and onion juice for hair.** No good evidence; onion can irritate the scalp.

**Neem (dogoyaro) and bitter leaf.** Traditional uses are many, but leaves and oils cause contact allergy in some people and should not be used on broken skin or on babies.

**Tea tree oil.** Modest evidence in mild acne when diluted to 5%. Undiluted oil causes burns and allergy.

## Which are harmful?

- **Lime, lemon and other citrus:** chemical burns and sun-triggered dark streaks.
- **Toothpaste on pimples:** irritant burns and dark marks.
- **Baking soda:** too alkaline; damages the skin barrier.
- **Undiluted apple cider vinegar:** acid burns.
- **Garlic, ginger and pepper pastes:** blistering.
- **Bleach, antiseptic liquid and medicated soap for daily bathing:** dryness, eczema and irritation.
- **Kerosene, petrol, engine oil, battery fluid and brake fluid** on ringworm, scabies, wounds or lice: burns, poisoning and scarring.
- **Hot water, hot stones or hot objects** on boils and rashes: burns.
- **Sulphur ointments and "native" mixtures of unknown content:** unpredictable.
- **Olive oil on babies' skin:** studies suggest it weakens the barrier in infants.
- **Herbal concoctions taken by mouth "for the skin":** risk to the liver and kidneys.
- **Mixed creams** sold in markets as natural or organic lighteners: many contain steroids, hydroquinone or mercury. Read [Is skin lightening safe?](/blog/is-skin-lightening-safe/)

## How do I use the useful ones well?

1. **Patch-test first:** apply a little to the inner forearm for two days.
2. **One new thing at a time.**
3. **Clean hands, clean container.** Scoop shea butter with a spoon, not fingers.
4. **Damp skin** for butters and oils, straight after bathing.
5. **Not on broken, infected or weeping skin.**
6. **Stop** at the first sign of burning, swelling or rash.

## When is a remedy not enough?

Remedies can moisturise and soothe. They do not treat fungal infections, scabies, moderate acne, eczema flares, melasma or hair loss. Using them for weeks while a condition spreads is the most common harm of all.
