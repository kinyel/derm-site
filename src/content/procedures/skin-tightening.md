## How it works

Collagen fibres shorten when heated to a certain temperature, and the skin responds to that heat by making new collagen. Tightening treatments deliver heat to the deeper layer of skin while protecting the surface. Radiofrequency does this with electrical energy; [radiofrequency microneedling](/services/aesthetic-treatments/microneedling/) delivers it through fine needles for a stronger effect.

## What it can and cannot do

**It can** firm mild looseness along the jawline, cheeks, neck, around the eyes, and on the abdomen, upper arms and thighs, and soften fine lines.

**It cannot** remove excess skin, lift heavy jowls or replace lost volume. Marked sagging needs surgery, and hollows need [fillers](/services/aesthetic-treatments/dermal-fillers/). The doctor will tell you if tightening is unlikely to help.

## Who it is for

Adults with early skin laxity, often from the late thirties, and people whose skin has loosened after weight loss or pregnancy. Good general health and stable weight give better results.

Not suitable with a pacemaker or implanted electrical device, metal implants in the treated area, pregnancy, active skin infection or recent filler in the same area.

## On brown and Black skin

Darker skin holds its firmness longer because melanin limits sun damage to collagen. When laxity does appear it tends to affect the mid-face and jawline. Radiofrequency is "colour-blind", so it is preferred over light-based tightening on dark skin.

## Before, during and after

**Before.** A consultation to examine skin quality and agree realistic aims. Arrive with clean skin and remove jewellery near the area.

**During.** A gel is applied and the handpiece is moved over the skin, which feels warm to hot. A session takes 30 to 60 minutes. Radiofrequency microneedling needs numbing cream first.

**After.** Mild redness or swelling for a few hours. Normal activities can resume immediately. A course is usually three to six treatments, three to four weeks apart.

## Risks and how they are managed

Temporary redness, swelling or tenderness are common. Burns, blisters, dents from fat loss and dark marks are uncommon and are prevented by monitoring skin temperature and using suitable settings.
