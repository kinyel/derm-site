## Why employers ask for it

Skin disease is one of the most common work-related health problems worldwide, and most of it affects the hands. Sore, cracked hands slow work, raise the risk of infection in food and healthcare settings, and can force skilled people out of a job. Much of it is preventable when it is noticed early.

Screening also picks up conditions unrelated to work that staff have been putting off: fungal infections, acne, eczema, hair and scalp problems and the occasional lesion that needs prompt attention.

## What the programme can include

- **On-site or in-clinic skin checks** for groups of staff
- **Occupational assessment:** how tasks, substances, gloves and washing routines affect the skin
- **[Patch testing](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/patch-testing/)** at the clinic for employees with suspected allergic contact dermatitis
- **Education sessions:** hand care, sun protection for outdoor workers, safe use of skin products, recognising warning signs
- **A report for the employer** with anonymised findings and practical recommendations
- **Follow-up** for staff who need treatment, in person or by [online consultation](/services/dermatology-consultations/online-consultation/)

## How it is arranged

1. **Enquiry.** Tell us the size of the team, the type of work and what prompted the request.
2. **Plan.** We propose a format, dates and what is needed on site: a private room, good light and a schedule.
3. **Screening day.** Staff are seen individually. Each person leaves with written advice.
4. **Report.** The employer receives the summary and recommendations.

## Screening on brown and Black skin

Early dermatitis shows as dryness, darkening, greyness and fine cracks more than redness on darker skin, so it is often missed until it is advanced. Outdoor workers with albinism need particular attention to sun protection and regular checks for skin cancer.

## Limits

A screening check is brief and does not replace a full consultation. Its purpose is to find people who need one and to reduce risks at work. Employees with findings are advised what to do next.
