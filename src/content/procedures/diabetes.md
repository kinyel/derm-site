## What diabetes is

Insulin is the hormone that moves sugar from the blood into cells, where it is used for energy. In diabetes there is too little insulin, or the body does not respond to it, and sugar builds up in the blood. Over years, high blood sugar damages small and large blood vessels and nerves.

The main types are:

- **Type 2 diabetes:** the great majority of cases in adults.
- **Type 1 diabetes:** needs insulin from the start.
- **Gestational diabetes:** appears in pregnancy and raises the mother's later risk of type 2.
- **Prediabetes:** blood sugar above normal but below the diabetes range; a warning and an opportunity.

Pooled studies suggest that roughly one adult in seventeen in Nigeria has diabetes, with higher rates in cities, and that many are undiagnosed.

## Who is at higher risk

- A parent, brother or sister with diabetes
- Excess weight, especially around the waist
- Little physical activity
- Age over 40
- High blood pressure or high cholesterol
- Previous gestational diabetes, or a baby weighing over 4 kg
- Polycystic ovary syndrome
- Dark, velvety skin in the neck or armpits (acanthosis nigricans)

## Signs to act on

Thirst, frequent urination, tiredness, weight loss without trying, blurred vision, tingling or numbness in the feet, slow-healing sores, and recurring infections of the skin, gums, bladder or genitals.

The skin can give early warning. Darkening and thickening at the back of the neck, groin and underarms; multiple [skin tags](/conditions/skin-tags/); repeated [boils](/conditions/boils-and-abscesses/); and fungal rashes that keep returning all justify a blood sugar test. Read [Diabetes and your skin: signs to watch](/blog/diabetes-and-your-skin/) and [dark underarms, inner thighs, knees and neck](/conditions/dark-underarms-inner-thighs-knees-and-neck/).

## How it is diagnosed

| Test | What it measures | Diabetes range |
|---|---|---|
| Fasting plasma glucose | Sugar after 8 hours without food | 7.0 mmol/L (126 mg/dL) or higher |
| HbA1c | Average sugar over about 3 months | 6.5% (48 mmol/mol) or higher |
| Random plasma glucose | Sugar at any time, with symptoms | 11.1 mmol/L (200 mg/dL) or higher |
| Oral glucose tolerance test | Sugar 2 hours after a glucose drink | 11.1 mmol/L (200 mg/dL) or higher |

HbA1c can be misleading in people with sickle cell trait or disease and some forms of anaemia, which are common in Nigeria, so the doctor may rely on glucose tests instead.

## How our diabetes clinic in Lagos manages your care

Good care aims at more than a sugar number. It reduces the risk of heart attack, stroke, kidney failure, blindness and amputation.

1. **Education.** Understanding what raises and lowers your sugar is the foundation.
2. **Food.** Regular meals, smaller portions of starchy staples such as rice, yam, garri, bread and pounded foods, more vegetables and beans, fewer sugary drinks. Nigerian meals can be adapted without being abandoned.
3. **Activity.** At least 150 minutes a week, plus less time sitting.
4. **Weight.** Even a modest loss improves blood sugar and blood pressure.
5. **Medicine.** Tablets, and for some people injections including insulin, chosen by a doctor for your type of diabetes, kidney function and other conditions.
6. **Blood pressure and cholesterol** control, which protect the heart and kidneys as much as sugar control does. See [hypertension](/services/internal-medicine/hypertension/) and [raised cholesterol](/services/internal-medicine/high-cholesterol/).
7. **Regular checks:** HbA1c, kidney tests, urine protein, cholesterol, blood pressure, feet and a referral for eye screening.

This page does not advise you to start, stop or change any medicine. Herbal remedies sold as cures for diabetes are not a substitute for treatment, and some interact with medicines; tell your doctor about anything you take.

## Feet and skin: where the dermatology team helps

Diabetes can numb the feet and reduce their blood supply. A blister or crack that is not felt can turn into a [diabetic foot ulcer](/conditions/diabetic-foot-ulcers/) within days. The clinic's [foot skin care](/services/foot-skin-care/) and [wound care](/services/wound-care/) services treat dry and cracked skin, fungal infection, calluses and wounds, and teach daily foot care.

**Every day:** look at both feet, including the soles and between the toes; wash and dry them carefully; moisturise but not between the toes; never walk barefoot; check inside shoes before putting them on.

> [!DOCTOR] See a doctor soon if
> - you have symptoms of diabetes or several risk factors and have never been tested
> - you have diabetes and have not had a review, eye check or foot check in the past year
> - your home readings are often above the range your doctor set
> - you have any break in the skin of a foot

> [!URGENT] Urgent care
> Go to a hospital emergency department for very high blood sugar with vomiting, stomach pain, deep or rapid breathing, drowsiness or confusion; for low blood sugar that does not improve after sugar, or with fainting or fits; and for a foot that is hot, swollen, black, foul-smelling or newly numb.
