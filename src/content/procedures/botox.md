## What Botox is

"Botox" is the best-known brand name for botulinum toxin type A, a purified protein that blocks the signal between a nerve and a muscle. Injected in very small amounts, it stops the treated muscle from contracting fully. The skin over that muscle no longer creases, so the line softens. People use "Botox" for all brands of this medicine, and this page does the same.

It treats **dynamic lines**, the ones made by movement: frown lines between the eyebrows, horizontal forehead lines and the crow's feet beside the eyes. It does not fill hollows or replace lost volume; that is what [dermal fillers](/services/aesthetic-treatments/dermal-fillers/) do. Our article [Botox or fillers: what's the difference?](/blog/botox-or-fillers/) compares the two.

## Who it is for

- Adults bothered by frown lines, forehead lines or crow's feet that show at rest.
- People with [excessive sweating](/conditions/hyperhidrosis/) of the underarms that has not responded to antiperspirants.
- Less often, a dermatologist uses it for a gummy smile, a dimpled chin, neck bands or jaw-muscle bulk. These need careful assessment.

It is not suitable if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a skin infection at the injection site, have a nerve or muscle disorder such as myasthenia gravis, or have reacted to botulinum toxin before. Tell the doctor about every medicine you take, including blood thinners and antibiotics.

## Botox on brown and Black skin

The medicine works on muscle, so skin tone does not change how well it works. What does differ is the pattern of ageing. Melanin protects against sun damage, so deep wrinkles tend to appear ten or more years later in dark skin, and the forehead and frown lines are usually the first to show. Many people with dark skin are more concerned about uneven tone, dark marks or loss of volume than about lines, and those need different treatments.

A needle prick can leave a pinpoint dark mark in skin that pigments easily. Using fine needles, few entry points and avoiding picking keeps this to a minimum, and any marks fade over weeks.

## Before, during and after

**Before.** At the consultation the doctor asks what bothers you, watches how your face moves and checks your medical history. You are told what the treatment can and cannot do, and you can take time to decide. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours beforehand, and ask your own doctor before stopping any prescribed blood thinner.

**During.** The skin is cleaned. The doctor asks you to frown, raise your brows and smile, then places a series of tiny injections into the chosen muscles. Each feels like a brief pinprick. A face treatment takes about 10 to 15 minutes. For underarm sweating, a numbing cream is applied first and the injections are spread evenly across the area.

**After.** Small bumps at the injection points settle within half an hour. You can return to work straight away. Results begin after a few days and are assessed at two weeks, when a small adjustment can be made if one side is stronger than the other.

## Risks and how they are managed

| Possible effect | How often | What is done |
|---|---|---|
| Pinprick redness, slight swelling | Common, lasts under an hour | Nothing needed |
| Bruising | Occasional | Pressure at the time; fades in a week |
| Headache | Occasional, first day or two | Paracetamol if needed |
| Heavy brow or drooping eyelid | Uncommon | Careful placement prevents most; settles as the effect wears off, and eye drops can help meanwhile |
| Uneven smile or brows | Uncommon | Reviewed at two weeks and balanced |
| Allergy, difficulty swallowing or breathing | Rare | Urgent medical care |

The most serious problems reported with botulinum toxin come from fake, diluted or unlicensed products and from injectors without medical training. Ask which brand will be used. You are entitled to see the vial and its registration details before it is opened.

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> Do not have Botox at a salon, a party or at home, or from anyone who cannot tell you the product name and their medical qualification. Do not buy botulinum toxin online.

## How often it is repeated

Treatment is repeated when movement returns, usually every three to four months, and no sooner than three months. Some people find that lines soften over time and choose longer gaps. If you stop, the muscles simply return to normal.
