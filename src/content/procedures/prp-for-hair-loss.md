## What PRP hair treatment is

Platelets are the cell fragments in blood that start clotting and release growth factors at a wound. To make platelet-rich plasma, about 10 to 20 millilitres of blood is taken from your arm and spun in a centrifuge. The layer of plasma with concentrated platelets is drawn off and injected into the scalp at the level of the hair roots.

In the laboratory, these growth factors prolong the growth phase of the hair cycle and improve the blood supply around follicles. PRP is your own tissue, so it cannot cause an allergy.

## Diagnosis first

PRP is not a treatment for "hair loss" in general. It has evidence in some types and not in others, so the dermatologist first confirms the cause with a scalp examination, [trichoscopy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/trichoscopy/) and, where needed, blood tests.

| Type of hair loss | Place of PRP |
|---|---|
| [Female pattern hair loss](/conditions/female-pattern-hair-loss/) | Supported by trials; used with medical treatment |
| [Male pattern hair loss](/conditions/male-pattern-hair-loss/) | Supported by trials; used with medical treatment |
| [Alopecia areata](/conditions/alopecia-areata/) | Some evidence; steroid injections remain first choice |
| [Traction alopecia](/conditions/traction-alopecia/) | Limited evidence; stopping the pulling is the treatment |
| [Telogen effluvium](/conditions/telogen-effluvium/) | Usually recovers on its own once the trigger is treated |
| Scarring alopecia | Not a treatment; inflammation must be controlled |

Low iron, thyroid disease and scalp conditions are treated first, because PRP cannot overcome them. See [hair and scalp](/services/hair-and-scalp/) for the full range of treatments.

## PRP on African-textured hair and dark skin

Hair loss in Black women is often a mixture: traction at the edges, pattern thinning at the crown and sometimes early scarring. PRP will not help the scarred component, which is why a careful diagnosis matters before paying for a course. Injections in the scalp do not usually leave visible marks. Come with hair loose or in a style that exposes the scalp; injections cannot be given properly through a weave, wig cap or tight braids.

## Before, during and after

**Before.** Eat and drink normally. Avoid anti-inflammatory painkillers such as ibuprofen for a few days beforehand unless they are prescribed, because they affect platelets. Wash your hair on the day and leave it free of oils and sprays.

**During.** Blood is taken and prepared, which takes about 15 minutes. The scalp is cleaned and numbed, then PRP is injected in a grid across the thinning area with a fine needle. The appointment lasts 45 to 60 minutes.

**After.** Mild tenderness, tightness or a dull headache can last a day or two. Do not wash your hair until the next day, and avoid dyes, relaxers, heat styling and tight styles for a few days. Paracetamol can be taken; avoid anti-inflammatory painkillers for a few days.

## Risks and how they are managed

- **Pain, tenderness, pinpoint bleeding:** common and brief.
- **Swelling of the forehead** for a day or two after frontal injections.
- **Headache.**
- **Infection:** rare with sterile preparation and technique.
- **Temporary increase in shedding:** occasionally reported in the first weeks.
- **No benefit:** a real possibility, assessed at six months.

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> PRP should be prepared and injected in a clinic, with sterile single-use equipment, by a medical team. Blood handled in salons or spas carries a risk of infection.

## How PRP fits with other treatments

Medical regrowth treatment applied to the scalp or taken by mouth remains the foundation for pattern hair loss. PRP and [scalp microneedling](/services/aesthetic-treatments/microneedling/) are additions. Where hair has gone completely, a [hair transplant](/services/hair-and-scalp/hair-transplant/) may be discussed.
