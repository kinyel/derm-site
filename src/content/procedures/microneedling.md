## How it works

A motorised pen moves a cluster of fine needles in and out of the skin thousands of times a minute, to a depth the doctor sets for each area. The punctures are too small to scar, but they are enough to start the skin's repair process. Over the following weeks new collagen and elastin form, lifting shallow scars and firming the surface.

**Radiofrequency (RF) microneedling** adds heat at the needle tips. The heat acts in the deeper skin while the surface stays relatively cool.

## What it treats

- [Acne scars](/conditions/acne-scars/)
- [Enlarged pores](/conditions/enlarged-pores/) and rough texture
- [Pregnancy and growth stretch marks](/conditions/stretch-marks/) on the abdomen, thighs, hips and arms
- Fine lines and mild skin laxity
- Surgical and injury scars once fully healed
- The scalp, as an addition to treatment for [pattern hair loss](/services/hair-and-scalp/)

## On brown and Black skin

Microneedling does not rely on light or heat at the surface, so it is among the safer resurfacing options for darker skin and is widely used for acne scars in skin of colour. The remaining risk is post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation from needling too deeply or exposing the skin to sun afterwards. Preparation with sunscreen, sensible depths and four-week gaps keep that risk low. If you form [keloids](/conditions/keloids/), say so; treatment may be limited to the face, where keloids are rare, or avoided.

## Before, during and after

**Before.** Acne should be controlled first. Stop retinoids and acids five days ahead. Tell the doctor about cold sores, blood thinners, pregnancy and acne tablets taken in the last six months.

**During.** After numbing and antiseptic cleansing, the pen is passed over each area several times. Pinpoint bleeding is normal. A face takes 20 to 30 minutes after numbing.

**After.** Redness and tightness for one to three days, then light flaking. For 24 hours use only water on the skin; for a week avoid sun, sweat, swimming, make-up brushes that are not clean, and active skincare.

## Risks and how they are managed

- **Redness, swelling, pinpoint scabs:** expected.
- **Dark marks:** uncommon; treated with pigment creams and sun protection.
- **Infection or cold sore flare:** rare; sterile single-use cartridges, and preventive tablets if you are prone.
- **Track marks or scarring:** avoided by correct depth and gentle pressure.
- **Burns with RF microneedling:** rare; prevented by correct settings.

> [!DERM] Contact the clinic if
> - redness or swelling is worse after day three
> - you see pus, crusting or blisters
> - new dark patches appear in the weeks afterwards

## Combining treatments

Microneedling can be paired with [PRP](/services/aesthetic-treatments/prp-microneedling/), and newer products such as [exosomes](/services/aesthetic-treatments/exosome-therapy/) or [PDRN](/services/aesthetic-treatments/pdrn-treatment/). The evidence for each addition is set out on its own page.
