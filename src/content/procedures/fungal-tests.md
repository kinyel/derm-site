## Why testing matters in Lagos

Heat and humidity make fungal infections very common here, and antifungal and "triple-action" creams are sold freely. Many people arrive after weeks of a mixed steroid cream, with a rash that has spread, lost its typical ring shape and become harder to recognise. Dermatologists call this tinea incognito. A test settles the question.

Tablets for scalp and nail infection are taken for weeks to months and need a confirmed diagnosis first. Roughly half of abnormal-looking nails are not fungal at all.

## The tests

- **Skin scraping:** scale from the active edge of a rash.
- **Nail clipping and scraping:** from the crumbly part of the nail and the debris under it.
- **Hair pluck or scalp brushing:** for suspected scalp ringworm in children.
- **KOH microscopy:** potassium hydroxide dissolves skin cells so that fungal threads and spores can be seen.
- **Fungal culture:** grows the fungus to identify its species, which guides the choice of medicine and, for scalp infection, points to the likely source.
- **Wood's lamp:** an ultraviolet lamp under which some fungal and bacterial infections glow.
- **Bacterial swab and culture:** when infection with bacteria is suspected as well.

## On brown and Black skin

Ringworm on dark skin is often brown or grey with a slightly raised, scaly edge, and it leaves dark marks. [Pityriasis versicolor](/conditions/pityriasis-versicolor/) shows as pale or dark patches on the chest and back. Both can be confused with vitiligo, eczema or [seborrhoeic dermatitis](/conditions/seborrhoeic-dermatitis-and-dandruff/); a scraping distinguishes them quickly.

## Before the test

- Stop antifungal creams, powders and shampoos for two weeks if you can, and tell the doctor what you have used.
- Do not apply any cream on the day.
- Do not cut the affected nail short beforehand, and remove nail polish.

## After the test

No aftercare is needed. If the result is positive, the dermatologist prescribes the appropriate antifungal and advises how to stop it returning. See [ringworm and other fungal skin infections](/conditions/ringworm-and-fungal-skin-infections/) and [fungal nail infection](/conditions/fungal-nail-infection/).
