## What makes a facial "medical"

The steps look familiar: cleanse, exfoliate, extract, treat, protect. The difference is in who plans them and what is used. The dermatologist first works out what your skin needs, so that the facial supports your treatment instead of irritating it. A facial that suits oily, acne-prone skin can harm skin with eczema or melasma.

## Types of medical facial

- **Deep-cleansing:** removes oil, sunscreen residue and dead cells; blackheads and whiteheads are extracted with sterile tools.
- **Acne:** deep cleansing with a salicylic acid step and calming ingredients; often paired with blue [LED light](/services/laser-treatments/led-and-ipl/).
- **Hydrating:** for dry or dehydrated skin and for skin recovering from harmattan, retinoids or procedures.
- **Brightening:** gentle exfoliation and pigment-calming ingredients for dull skin and uneven tone.
- **Anti-ageing:** exfoliation, antioxidants and massage for fine lines and loss of glow.

A facial may lead into a [chemical peel](/services/aesthetic-treatments/chemical-peels/) or [microneedling](/services/aesthetic-treatments/microneedling/) when more is needed.

## On brown and Black skin

Rough extraction, harsh scrubs and steam that is too hot all inflame the skin, and inflamed dark skin leaves dark marks. A medical facial for darker skin is therefore gentle: mild exfoliating acids in place of gritty scrubs, careful extraction of non-inflamed spots only, and sunscreen at the end.

## Before, during and after

**Before.** Stop retinoids and exfoliating acids two to three days ahead, and do not wax or shave the face the day before. Tell the clinician about any new products, cold sores or pregnancy.

**During.** About 45 to 60 minutes. You may feel tingling from exfoliating steps and brief discomfort during extraction.

**After.** Mild redness settles within hours. Avoid make-up for the rest of the day, and sun, heat and active products for two days. Use moisturiser and sunscreen.

## Risks and how they are managed

Temporary redness, a few spots surfacing in the following days, irritation from a product, or dark marks if the skin is picked. Tell the clinic if stinging, swelling or a rash lasts beyond a day.
