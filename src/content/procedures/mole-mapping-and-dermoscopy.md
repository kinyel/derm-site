## What happens at a mole check

The dermatologist asks about your skin history, sun exposure, family history and any spot you are worried about. You undress to your underwear and the skin is examined from scalp to soles, including between the toes, the nails and, if you wish, the genital area. Each mole or spot of interest is examined with the dermatoscope.

The whole check takes 15 to 30 minutes. You are told the result straight away.

## What mole mapping adds

For people with many moles it is impossible to remember what each one looked like. Mole mapping records them:

1. **Overview photographs** of each body region show where every mole sits.
2. **Close-up dermoscopic images** are stored for moles that need watching.
3. **At the next visit** the images are compared, so a new mole or a change in an old one is picked up.

Change over time is the most reliable sign of an early melanoma, and mapping makes change visible. It also means fewer moles are removed "just in case".

## On brown and Black skin

Moles are fewer on dark skin, and melanoma is uncommon, but when it occurs it is usually found late. It tends to arise where there is little pigment or sun: the palms, soles, under the nails and inside the mouth. A proper check on dark skin always includes those sites.

Things that deserve a look:

- a dark patch on a palm or sole that is growing, uneven in colour or wider than a pencil's eraser
- a dark band in one nail that is widening, or pigment spreading onto the skin around the nail
- a sore on an old scar, burn or ulcer
- any mole that looks different from your others

Dermoscopy patterns on palms and soles differ from those elsewhere, and reading them correctly takes training. Small raised dark spots on the cheeks are usually harmless [dermatosis papulosa nigra](/conditions/dermatosis-papulosa-nigra/).

## If something looks suspicious

The dermatologist explains the concern and recommends removing the spot or taking a sample. This is a [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) or [mole removal](/services/skin-surgery/mole-removal/), done under local anaesthetic and sent to a laboratory.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist promptly if a mole or spot
> - changes in size, shape or colour
> - has more than one colour or an irregular edge
> - itches, bleeds, crusts or does not heal within four weeks
> - is new and you are over 30

## Before your appointment

Remove nail polish and make-up, avoid body shimmer or fake tan, and wear your hair loose so that the scalp can be examined.
