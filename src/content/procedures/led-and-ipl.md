## LED light therapy

Light-emitting diodes give off low-energy light of one colour. The skin absorbs it without heating up.

- **Blue light** (around 415 nanometres) acts on acne bacteria in the pores.
- **Red light** (around 630 nanometres) reaches deeper, calms inflammation and supports healing; it is used after procedures and for inflamed [acne](/conditions/acne/).
- **Near-infrared** reaches deeper still and is used for healing.

A treatment takes 15 to 20 minutes under a panel, with eye shields. There is no downtime. Benefits build over a course, typically twice a week for four to six weeks. LED is often added to a [medical facial](/services/aesthetic-treatments/medical-facials/).

## IPL (intense pulsed light)

IPL is a filtered flash lamp, not a laser. It emits many wavelengths at once and is used for sun spots, redness, fine vessels and hair reduction. Because it is not selective, it suits light skin best. On brown and Black skin the clinic uses lasers with a single, longer wavelength instead; see [laser hair removal](/services/laser-treatments/laser-hair-removal/) and [laser vascular treatment](/services/laser-treatments/laser-vascular-treatment/).

## Photodynamic therapy (PDT)

PDT has three steps: the lesion is gently scraped, a light-sensitising cream is applied and left for a few hours, and a red or blue light is shone on the area for several minutes. Treated cells are destroyed and the skin heals over one to two weeks. It is a medical treatment for precancerous sun damage and selected thin skin cancers, which are uncommon in dark skin but occur in people with albinism and others at higher risk. See [skin cancer care](/services/skin-cancer-care/).

## Not the same as phototherapy

Ultraviolet light treatment for vitiligo, psoriasis and eczema is a different service. See [phototherapy](/services/clinical-dermatology/phototherapy/).

## Risks

LED: essentially none beyond eye protection. IPL: burns, blisters and pigment change, particularly on darker skin. PDT: pain during the light, redness, swelling, crusting and light sensitivity for 48 hours.
