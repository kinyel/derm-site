## What the service covers

The doctor assesses adults who have symptoms that suggest an infection, such as fever, chills, sweats, cough, sore throat, burning on passing urine, diarrhoea, or a wound or rash that is hot and spreading. The aim is to identify what is causing the illness, treat it correctly and recognise the cases that need hospital care.

The specific conditions managed at the clinic are explained at your consultation.

## How an infection is assessed

1. **History.** When symptoms began, how they have changed, travel, contacts who are unwell, food and water, medicines already taken and your vaccination history.
2. **Examination.** Temperature, pulse, blood pressure, breathing and a check of the throat, chest, abdomen, skin and glands.
3. **Tests, where they will change treatment.** Blood tests, urine tests, swabs or cultures to identify the organism and the medicine it responds to.
4. **Treatment.** The medicine, if one is needed, is chosen for the confirmed or most likely cause. Fluids, rest and fever control are part of care.
5. **Review.** You are told what improvement to expect and when to come back if it does not happen.

## Why testing before treatment matters in Lagos

It is common to treat a fever at home with medicines bought over the counter, sometimes several at once. This hides the real diagnosis, exposes people to side effects from medicines they did not need, and drives resistance. A short assessment and the right test usually give a clear answer the same day or within a few days.

## Antibiotics: used when they will help

Antibiotics work only against bacteria. They do nothing for colds, flu and most sore throats, which are caused by viruses. When an antibiotic is needed, the doctor chooses the type and length of course for that infection. This page does not advise anyone to start, stop or change a medicine; if you have side effects or questions about a course, contact the doctor.

## Infection and long-term conditions

People with [diabetes](/services/internal-medicine/diabetes/) get infections more easily, and infections raise blood sugar. Repeated infections can be the first sign that blood sugar is high. Good control of long-term conditions is part of preventing infection.

## Infections of the skin

Skin infections are common in Lagos's heat and humidity. The dermatology team treats [boils and abscesses](/conditions/boils-and-abscesses/), [cellulitis](/conditions/cellulitis/), [impetigo](/conditions/impetigo/), [shingles](/conditions/shingles/) and [fungal infections](/conditions/ringworm-and-fungal-skin-infections/). The internal medicine doctor is involved when a skin infection comes with fever or keeps returning.

## Preventing infection

- Wash hands with soap and water before eating and after using the toilet.
- Drink safe water and eat food that is freshly cooked and hot.
- Keep wounds clean and covered.
- Do not share razors, towels or clippers.
- Ask the doctor which vaccinations you are due.
- Complete any prescribed course as directed.

> [!URGENT] Urgent care
> Go to the nearest hospital emergency department for fever with confusion, drowsiness, a stiff neck, a rash that does not fade when pressed, difficulty breathing, chest pain, fits, persistent vomiting, very little urine, or a rapidly spreading area of red, hot, painful skin.
