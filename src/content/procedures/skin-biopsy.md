## Types of biopsy

| Type | How it is done | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Punch | A circular blade removes a core 3–6 mm wide; one or two stitches | Rashes, scalp and inflammatory conditions |
| Shave | A blade removes the raised part level with the skin; no stitches | Raised growths |
| Excisional | The whole lesion is cut out with a margin and stitched | Suspicious moles; small growths |
| Incisional | A wedge is taken from a large lesion | Large growths or ulcers |
| Scalp | A deeper punch that includes hair roots | Hair loss with scarring |
| Nail | A sample of nail plate, bed or matrix | Dark streaks, nail tumours, some nail diseases |

## What the laboratory does

**Histopathology** is the examination of stained tissue under a microscope. A **dermatopathologist** is a pathologist with special training in skin. **Direct immunofluorescence** is an extra test on a fresh sample that shows antibody deposits in blistering and autoimmune conditions such as [pemphigus](/conditions/pemphigus/), [pemphigoid](/conditions/pemphigoid/) and [lupus](/conditions/cutaneous-lupus/).

## On brown and Black skin

Many inflammatory conditions look alike on dark skin because redness is hard to see and they all tend to leave dark marks. A biopsy can separate them. It is especially useful for hair loss with scarring, which is common in women of African descent and needs early diagnosis; see [hair and scalp](/services/hair-and-scalp/).

## Before, during and after

**Before.** Tell the doctor about blood thinners, allergies to anaesthetic or dressings, bleeding problems and keloids. Do not apply creams to the area on the day.

**During.** The site is cleaned and numbed with a small injection that stings for a few seconds. The sample is taken in a minute or two. The wound is stitched or dressed. The visit takes about 15 to 20 minutes.

**After.** Mild soreness for a day or two; paracetamol is usually enough. Follow the wound care sheet you are given.

## Risks

Bleeding, bruising, infection, a scar, pigment change, and occasionally a sample that does not give a clear answer, in which case a second biopsy may be advised.

> [!DERM] Contact the clinic if
> - bleeding does not stop after 15 minutes of firm pressure
> - redness, swelling, pain or pus develop
> - stitches come out early
