## What the service covers

**[General medical consultations](/services/internal-medicine/general-medical-consultation/)** are for adults with a new symptom, a health check, or a long-term condition that needs review.

**[High blood pressure](/services/internal-medicine/hypertension/)** usually causes no symptoms and is found only by measuring it. Untreated, it raises the risk of stroke, heart disease and kidney damage.

**[Diabetes](/services/internal-medicine/diabetes/)** raises blood sugar. Over time it affects the eyes, kidneys, nerves, blood vessels and skin.

**[High cholesterol](/services/internal-medicine/high-cholesterol/)** adds to the risk of heart attack and stroke and is assessed together with blood pressure, blood sugar, weight and smoking.

**[Infectious diseases](/services/internal-medicine/infectious-diseases/)** covers the assessment and treatment of infections in adults.

## Where skin and general health meet

Doctors often see the first sign of a medical condition on the skin:

- Dark, velvety thickening on the neck, underarms or groin (acanthosis nigricans) is linked to insulin resistance. See [dark underarms, inner thighs, knees and neck](/conditions/dark-underarms-inner-thighs-knees-and-neck/).
- Repeated [boils](/conditions/boils-and-abscesses/), fungal infections in skin folds and slow-healing cuts can point to undiagnosed diabetes.
- [Foot ulcers](/conditions/diabetic-foot-ulcers/) are a serious complication of diabetes and need both wound care and sugar control.
- Yellowish deposits on the eyelids can accompany high cholesterol.
- Some skin treatments, including certain tablets for acne, psoriasis and autoimmune conditions, affect blood pressure, blood fats or blood sugar and need monitoring.

Our article [Diabetes and your skin: signs to watch](/blog/diabetes-and-your-skin/) describes these in more detail.

## How care is organised

A first consultation takes a history, examines you and measures blood pressure, weight and, where relevant, blood sugar. Blood and urine tests give a baseline. You and the doctor agree a plan that covers diet, activity, medicines where needed, and how often you will be reviewed. Long-term conditions are managed through regular checks, because targets and treatment change over time.

This site does not tell you to start, stop or change any medicine. Those decisions are made with a doctor who knows your results.

> [!URGENT] Urgent care
> Call for emergency help or go to the nearest hospital emergency department for chest pain or pressure, sudden shortness of breath, weakness or numbness on one side, drooping of the face, slurred speech, sudden severe headache, confusion, fainting, a very high blood pressure reading with any of these symptoms, or very high or very low blood sugar with drowsiness, vomiting or rapid breathing.
