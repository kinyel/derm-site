## Assessment comes first

Every aesthetic treatment at the clinic starts with a consultation. The dermatologist asks what you would like to change, examines your skin, and checks for conditions that should be treated first, such as active acne, melasma, eczema or infection. You are told what each option can realistically do, how many treatments it involves, the downtime and the risks. No treatment is given on the day unless you have had time to consider it.

We do not publish before-and-after photographs or promise results. Skin responds differently from person to person, and an honest range is more useful than a best case.

## How the treatments are grouped

**Injectables.** [Botox](/services/aesthetic-treatments/botox/) relaxes the muscles that crease the forehead, frown and eye area, and is also used for excessive sweating. [Dermal fillers](/services/aesthetic-treatments/dermal-fillers/) restore or add volume to lips, cheeks, chin, jawline and the under-eye area. Our article explains the [difference between Botox and fillers](/blog/botox-or-fillers/).

**Body and intimate treatments.** [Butt filler](/services/aesthetic-treatments/butt-filler/), [labia filler](/services/aesthetic-treatments/labia-filler/) and the [O-shot](/services/aesthetic-treatments/o-shot/) are described on their own pages, with their risks and who is not suitable.

**Skin treatments.** [Medical facials](/services/aesthetic-treatments/medical-facials/) and [chemical peels](/services/aesthetic-treatments/chemical-peels/) treat congestion, dullness, uneven tone and dark marks at increasing depths.

**Microneedling and regenerative care.** [Microneedling](/services/aesthetic-treatments/microneedling/), with or without radiofrequency, prompts the skin to make collagen and is used for acne scars, pores and stretch marks. [PRP microneedling](/services/aesthetic-treatments/prp-microneedling/), [exosome therapy](/services/aesthetic-treatments/exosome-therapy/) and [PDRN](/services/aesthetic-treatments/pdrn-treatment/) are newer additions; the evidence for each is described on its page.

**Skin tightening.** [Skin tightening](/services/aesthetic-treatments/skin-tightening/) uses heat from radiofrequency or other energy to firm lax skin on the face and body.

## Aesthetic treatment on brown and Black skin

Darker skin ages well: lines appear later, and sun damage is less. The concerns people bring are more often uneven tone, dark marks, scars and volume change than wrinkles. The main risk of treatment is post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, a dark mark left by anything that inflames the skin, followed by keloid scarring in people prone to it.

For that reason the clinic prepares the skin before peels and needling, starts with conservative strengths, spaces treatments so the skin recovers fully, and insists on sunscreen throughout. If you have had [keloids](/conditions/keloids/), say so at your consultation.

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> Do not have injectables, peels or laser at a salon, at home or from anyone who is not medically qualified. Do not buy fillers or "fat-dissolving" injections online. Complications from these are among the hardest problems dermatologists are asked to correct.
