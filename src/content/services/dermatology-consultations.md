## Which consultation should you book?

**General consultation.** For any new concern with the skin, hair or nails: a rash, spots, itching, dark patches, a growth, hair thinning or a nail that has changed.

**Specialist consultation.** For conditions that are complex, long-standing or have not responded to treatment, such as severe acne, widespread psoriasis, blistering conditions or [hair loss that leaves scars](/services/hair-and-scalp/).

**Follow-up consultation.** A shorter appointment to check how treatment is working, adjust it and manage side effects. Many follow-ups can be done as an [online consultation](/services/dermatology-consultations/online-consultation/).

**Second opinion.** A fresh look at a diagnosis or plan you have been given elsewhere.

If you are not sure, tell us what the concern is when you [book an appointment](/book-appointment/) and we will place you in the right slot.

## What the dermatologist assesses

A skin assessment looks at more than the spot you came about. The dermatologist checks your skin type and how easily it marks or scars, how sensitive it is, how oily or dry it is, and whether sun or products have damaged it. On brown and Black skin, the pattern of pigment change often tells as much as the rash itself.

Where the answer is not clear from looking, the next step is a test. The clinic's [diagnostic tests](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/) include skin scrapings for fungus, swabs, biopsies, patch testing for allergy and trichoscopy for hair.

## Planning treatment and reviewing medicines

Treatment planning turns the diagnosis into steps you can follow: what to use, in what order, for how long and what to expect by when. A medication review goes through everything you take or apply, including herbal products and creams bought without a prescription, to find anything that is causing or worsening the condition. Steroid and bleaching creams are a common cause of [skin damage](/conditions/skin-damage-from-bleaching-and-steroid-creams/) in Lagos, and many people do not know their cream contains one.

## Skin care for older adults

Skin thins and dries with age, heals more slowly and reacts more to medicines. Older adults are also more likely to have itching without a rash, leg ulcers and skin growths that need checking. A geriatric dermatology consultation allows time for these and for a review of all current medicines.

## For employers

Workplaces with exposure to chemicals, heat, wet work or protective equipment see more hand dermatitis and skin infection. The clinic offers [corporate skin screening](/services/dermatology-consultations/corporate-skin-screening/) for staff, at the clinic or on site.
