## What heat rash looks like on brown and Black skin

There are three depths of blockage:

- **Crystallina:** pinhead, clear blisters that break easily, without itch. Common on a newborn's forehead and neck and after a fever.
- **Rubra (prickly heat):** small, itchy bumps. On dark skin they look skin-coloured, dark brown or greyish, and the "red" of the name is hard to see. The skin feels rough, like sandpaper, and prickles when you start to sweat.
- **Profunda:** firm, flesh-coloured bumps after repeated attacks, with little itch.

It favours the neck, upper chest, back, waistband, armpits, groin and under the breasts; in babies, wherever clothing or a carrier keeps the skin warm.

## How to spot it

- It followed heat, heavy sweating, a fever or overdressing.
- Bumps are tiny and all look alike.
- They are not centred on hairs (bumps around hairs suggest [folliculitis](/conditions/folliculitis/)).
- Cooling down eases the prickling within an hour.

Weals that move around and fade within a day are [hives](/conditions/hives/).

## Causes and triggers

Sweat ducts become plugged by swollen skin cells and bacteria when the skin stays wet and hot. Sweat then escapes into the surrounding skin. Lagos humidity slows evaporation, power cuts remove fans and air-conditioning, and oils or thick ointments applied to babies seal the duct openings.

> [!DERM] See a doctor if
> - bumps fill with pus, crust or become painful
> - a baby has a fever, is feeding poorly or is unusually sleepy
> - the rash has not improved after a week of cooling
> - you feel dizzy, sick or stop sweating in the heat

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Petroleum jelly, shea butter and heavy oils on affected skin
> - Wrapping babies in several layers
> - Steroid and "triple action" creams for weeks
> - Scrubbing or hot baths

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

Examination is enough. The dermatologist checks for infection and for conditions that mimic it. An [online consultation](/services/dermatology-consultations/online-consultation/) with clear photographs is often suitable.

## Heat rash treatment

1. **Cool the skin:** a fan or air-conditioning, cool (not cold) baths, and time without clothes for babies.
2. **Dress lightly** in loose cotton.
3. **Soothe:** calamine or a light lotion; avoid greasy products.
4. **Treat itch or infection** with a short course of prescribed cream if needed.

Babies and children are seen within [children's skin care](/services/childrens-skin-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Keep bedrooms ventilated; use a fan when power allows.
- Bathe after heavy sweating and pat dry.
- Dress babies in one more thin layer than an adult would wear, no more.
- Use light, water-based moisturisers in the hot season.
