## What telogen effluvium looks like on African hair

There are no patches. Hair density falls everywhere, and the temples, where hair is naturally finer, look emptiest. In coiled hair, shed hairs stay tangled in the style until wash day or take-down, so the amount that comes out at once can be alarming: handfuls after removing braids that have been in for weeks. Counting is more reliable than impressions; losing up to about a hundred hairs a day is normal, and a take-down releases weeks of normal shedding together.

## How to spot it

- Shedding began suddenly, and you can recall an illness, delivery, operation, diet or upheaval two to four months earlier.
- Fallen hairs are long, with a tiny pale bulb at the root end.
- The scalp does not itch, hurt or scale.
- A gentle pull on a small bunch of hair removes several hairs from every part of the scalp.

Short broken pieces point to [hair breakage](/conditions/hair-breakage/). Smooth round patches point to [alopecia areata](/conditions/alopecia-areata/).

## Causes and triggers

Each hair grows for years, rests for a few months, then sheds. Normally about one in ten hairs is resting. Fever, blood loss, hormonal change after delivery, strict dieting, low iron, thyroid disease, some medicines and major stress move a much larger share into the resting phase together. In Lagos, febrile illnesses such as malaria and typhoid are frequent triggers.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - shedding has continued for more than six months
> - there is no obvious trigger
> - you have heavy periods, tiredness, weight change or feel cold or hot all the time
> - you see patches, scale or scarring
> - the hair loss is causing you distress

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Tight protective styles to "save" the hair; they add traction
> - Not washing or combing for fear of loss; resting hairs will shed regardless
> - Starting several supplements at once
> - Crash diets

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The dermatologist takes a timeline of the past six months, examines the scalp with [trichoscopy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/trichoscopy/) and performs a hair pull test. Blood tests commonly include a blood count, iron stores and thyroid function, arranged with the [internal medicine service](/services/internal-medicine/general-medical-consultation/) when a wider medical review is useful.

## Telogen effluvium treatment at the clinic

- **Correct the cause:** replace iron if stores are low, treat thyroid disease, adjust a medicine with your prescribing doctor, restore a balanced diet.
- **Wait with confidence.** Photographs every two months show progress more clearly than daily checking.
- **Minoxidil** may be offered when shedding is prolonged or pattern hair loss is also present.
- **Gentle care:** loose styles, minimal heat, wide-tooth combs.

For shedding after delivery, read [Is hair loss after childbirth normal?](/blog/is-hair-loss-after-childbirth-normal/) The [hair and scalp](/services/hair-and-scalp/) service provides follow-up.

## Prevention and daily care

- Eat enough protein and iron, especially after illness and while breastfeeding.
- Lose weight gradually.
- Treat fevers promptly and complete prescribed treatment.
