## What ichthyosis looks like on brown and Black skin

Scale shows up clearly against dark skin as a grey-white, ashy network, like dried river mud or fish scales, most striking on the shins. The scales are small, polygonal and stuck down at the centre with lifted edges. Skin folds such as the elbows, knees and armpits are usually spared. Palms show deep, numerous lines. In rarer forms, scales are large, dark brown and plate-like, or the baby is born in a shiny membrane.

## How to spot it

- Dryness has been present since early childhood.
- It is on both sides of the body and worst on the legs.
- It improves in the rainy season and worsens in harmattan.
- Relatives have similar skin.

## Causes and triggers

In ichthyosis vulgaris, a change in the filaggrin gene reduces the skin's natural moisturising factors. The same change underlies many cases of [eczema](/conditions/eczema/) and [keratosis pilaris](/conditions/keratosis-pilaris/). Soap, hot water and dry air aggravate it.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - a child has persistent widespread scaling
> - moisturisers are not controlling the dryness
> - the skin is cracked, sore or infected
> - scaling begins in adult life

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Scrubbing with rough sponges
> - Medicated and antiseptic soaps
> - Long hot baths
> - Lightening creams to mask the ashy look

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The pattern and family history are usually enough. A [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) or genetic referral is arranged for unusual forms, and blood tests for adult-onset cases through the [internal medicine service](/services/internal-medicine/general-medical-consultation/).

## Ichthyosis treatment at the clinic

A routine of soaking, gentle removal of loosened scale with a soft cloth, and generous application of a keratolytic moisturiser twice daily. Strengths are adjusted for children. Severe forms may be considered for retinoid treatment under supervision. Children are seen within [skin care for children](/services/childrens-skin-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Moisturise within minutes of bathing, every day, all year.
- Use a soap substitute.
- Humidify air-conditioned rooms where possible.
