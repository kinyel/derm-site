## How brown and Black skin ages

The pattern differs from light skin. Instead of early crow's feet and fine wrinkling, expect:

- **Tone:** blotchy darkening of the cheeks, temples and around the eyes and mouth; [melasma](/conditions/melasma/).
- **Growths:** [dermatosis papulosa nigra](/conditions/dermatosis-papulosa-nigra/) and flat dark spots.
- **Volume:** flattening of the cheeks, deeper tear troughs and nose-to-mouth folds, and jowls, as fat pads descend and bone remodels.
- **Texture:** coarser pores and dullness.
- **Lines:** forehead and frown lines from muscle movement, later than in light skin.

## How to spot sun damage

Compare the face, neck and backs of the hands with skin that is always covered, such as the inner upper arm. The difference in tone and texture is the sun's contribution.

## Causes and triggers

Ultraviolet and visible light, smoking, pollution and repeated inflammation degrade collagen and elastin. Lagos sun is strong all year. Steroid-containing lightening products add thinning.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - you want a plan that suits your skin tone
> - dark patches are spreading
> - a rough, scaly or bleeding spot appears on sun-exposed skin
> - you have albinism

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Sun exposure without protection
> - Lightening creams
> - Injectables, peels or lasers from unqualified providers
> - Many active products started together

## How it is assessed at the clinic

A consultation identifies which changes matter to you and what is driving them: pigment, growths, movement lines, volume or texture. Spots are checked with a dermatoscope.

## Treatment at the clinic

- **Foundation:** sunscreen, a retinoid, an antioxidant and moisturiser.
- **Pigment and texture:** [chemical peels](/services/aesthetic-treatments/chemical-peels/), [microneedling](/services/aesthetic-treatments/microneedling/), and selected lasers.
- **Lines of expression:** [botulinum toxin](/services/aesthetic-treatments/botox/).
- **Volume:** [dermal fillers](/services/aesthetic-treatments/dermal-fillers/).
- **Laxity:** [skin tightening](/services/aesthetic-treatments/skin-tightening/).
- **Growths:** removal of DPN.

Treatments are staged, starting with the least invasive. See [aesthetic treatments](/services/aesthetic-treatments/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Sunscreen every morning; hat and shade at midday.
- Do not smoke.
- Sleep, eat vegetables and fruit, and limit sugar.
