## What warts look like on brown and Black skin

- **Common warts:** firm, rough domes on the fingers, backs of the hands, elbows and knees, skin-coloured to grey-brown, interrupting the normal skin lines. Paring the surface reveals black pinpoint dots, which are tiny clotted blood vessels.
- **Flat (plane) warts:** small, smooth, flat-topped spots, often lighter or darker than the surrounding skin, in clusters on the face, backs of the hands and shins. Shaving spreads them in lines across the beard area or legs.
- **Filiform warts:** narrow, finger-like spikes on the eyelids, lips and neck.
- **Periungual warts:** thickened, cracked skin around the nails.
- **Plantar warts** on the soles are described under [plantar warts](/conditions/plantar-warts/), and warts on genital skin under [genital warts](/conditions/genital-warts/).

Several other growths resemble warts, including [small dark spots on the face (DPN)](/conditions/dermatosis-papulosa-nigra/), [skin tags](/conditions/skin-tags/), [molluscum](/conditions/molluscum-contagiosum/) and, rarely in adults, skin cancer.

## How to spot it

- The surface is rough, like a tiny cauliflower.
- Skin lines go around the bump, not across it.
- Black dots appear when the surface is rubbed down.
- New ones appear nearby or where you scratch.

## Causes and triggers

Over a hundred types of HPV infect skin. The virus enters through tiny cuts and softened, wet skin, and takes weeks to months to produce a visible wart. Different types prefer different sites; those causing hand warts are not the types linked to cervical cancer.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - a wart is on the face, eyelid, lips or genitals
> - warts are multiplying, painful or bleeding
> - pharmacy treatment has not worked after three months
> - you have diabetes, poor circulation or weak immunity
> - a single warty growth appears in an adult over 40 and keeps growing

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Cutting, burning or tying off warts at home
> - Battery acid, caustic soda or herbal pastes; they cause deep burns and scars
> - Wart acids on the face or genitals
> - Biting or picking warts
> - Sharing razors and nail clippers

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

Warts are recognised by examination and dermoscopy. A growth that looks unusual is sampled ([skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/)).

## Wart removal at the clinic

The dermatologist matches treatment to the wart, the site and your skin:

- **Salicylic acid** at prescription strength, applied daily after soaking and filing, for hands and feet. It is slow but leaves the fewest marks.
- **Cryotherapy** with liquid nitrogen every two to three weeks.
- **Electrocautery or curettage** under local anaesthetic for filiform and stubborn warts.
- **Creams that prompt an immune response** for flat or resistant warts.

Expect several visits. On dark skin, temporary dark or pale marks are common whichever method is used, and patients prone to [keloids and raised scars](/conditions/keloids/) are treated with extra care. Procedures are part of [skin surgery](/services/skin-surgery/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Cover warts with a plaster when swimming or at the gym.
- Dry hands and feet well; wear slippers in shared bathrooms.
- Do not shave over warts; use a separate razor around them.
- Keep nails short and stop nail biting.
