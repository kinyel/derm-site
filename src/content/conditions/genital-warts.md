## What genital warts look like on brown and Black skin

Warts are flesh-coloured, brown or grey-black, soft, and range from pinhead dots to clusters with a bumpy surface. In men they appear on the shaft or head of the penis, scrotum, groin and around the anus; in women on the vulva, in the vagina or on the cervix, and around the anus.

Several normal or harmless findings are mistaken for warts: pearly penile papules (a neat row of tiny bumps around the head of the penis), small pale spots called Fordyce spots, [skin tags](/conditions/skin-tags/) and [molluscum](/conditions/molluscum-contagiosum/). A firm, ulcerated or bleeding growth needs prompt examination.

## How to spot it

- New soft bumps on genital or anal skin.
- They are painless and slowly increasing in number.
- A partner has had similar bumps.

## Causes and triggers

HPV types 6 and 11 cause most genital warts and spread by skin-to-skin contact during vaginal, anal or oral sex. Condoms reduce but do not remove the risk, because they do not cover all affected skin.

> [!DERM] See a doctor if
> - you notice new growths on genital or anal skin
> - a growth bleeds, ulcerates or is firm
> - you are pregnant and have warts
> - warts are present in a child, which always requires careful assessment

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Wart paints for hands and feet; they burn genital skin
> - Cutting, tying or burning at home
> - Herbal pastes and acids
> - Sex without a condom until treatment is complete

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

Examination is usually sufficient, in private and with a chaperone if you wish. The clinic is led by a woman; read about [our lead consultant dermatologist]({{leadPath}}). A [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) is taken from unusual growths. Tests for HIV, syphilis and other infections are offered.

## Genital warts treatment at the clinic

Options are a prescribed cream or solution used at home for several weeks, cryotherapy at intervals, or removal under local anaesthetic. The choice depends on the number, site and your preference. On dark skin, temporary lighter or darker marks at treated sites are common. Partners are advised to be checked. Care is confidential within [men's and women's skin health](/services/mens-and-womens-skin-health/).

## Prevention and daily care

- HPV vaccination protects against the types that cause most warts and cervical cancers.
- Use condoms.
- Attend cervical screening as advised.
