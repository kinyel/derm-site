## What venous disease looks like on brown and Black skin

Before an ulcer forms, the lower leg changes. Iron from leaked blood stains the skin dark brown to black around the ankle; this is often mistaken for a simple "dark leg". The skin becomes thick, tight and woody, narrowing the leg above the ankle, and itchy, scaly patches of varicose eczema appear. Small white scar-like areas dotted with dark points are a warning that the skin is fragile. The ulcer itself is shallow with sloping edges and a moist pink or yellow base.

## How to spot it

- The sore is in the "gaiter" area between calf and ankle.
- The ankle swells by evening and improves overnight.
- Raising the leg eases the ache.

Punched-out, very painful ulcers on the toes or shin with a cold foot suggest arterial disease. Ulcers over the ankles in a young adult may be due to sickle cell disease. Ulcers under the foot in diabetes are covered under [diabetic foot ulcers](/conditions/diabetic-foot-ulcers/).

## Causes and triggers

Damaged vein valves let blood pool; pressure forces fluid and blood cells into the skin, which becomes inflamed and breaks down after a minor knock.

> [!URGENT] Seek care the same day if
> - the leg becomes hot, more swollen and painful, or you have a fever
> - the ulcer smells, enlarges quickly or bleeds heavily

> [!DERM] See a doctor if
> - any leg sore has not healed in two weeks
> - the lower leg is darkening, hardening or itching

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Herbal pastes, antiseptic powders, antibiotic powders from capsules and engine oil
> - Tight bandages applied without assessment
> - Long periods standing still or sitting with legs down

## How it is assessed at the clinic

The ulcer is measured and photographed. Pulses and, where needed, a Doppler pressure measurement check arterial supply. Blood tests look for diabetes, anaemia and sickle cell disease through the [internal medicine service](/services/internal-medicine/general-medical-consultation/). A swab is taken only if infection is suspected, and a [biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) if the ulcer is atypical or slow.

## Leg ulcer treatment at the clinic

Weekly or twice-weekly visits for cleaning, removal of dead tissue, a suitable dressing and compression bandaging. Surrounding eczema is treated with moisturiser and a steroid ointment. Infection is treated with antibiotics. Referral for a vein scan and procedure is arranged where appropriate. See [wound care](/services/wound-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Wear compression stockings daily once healed.
- Walk regularly and move your ankles when seated.
- Raise your legs above hip level when resting.
- Moisturise the lower legs and protect them from knocks.
