## What folliculitis looks like on brown and Black skin

Each bump sits at a hair and is skin-coloured, dark brown or topped with a tiny yellow head. There are no blackheads, which separates it from [acne](/conditions/acne/). As bumps heal they leave dark dots, so the thighs, buttocks or back look speckled. Yeast folliculitis gives a crop of identical 1 to 2 millimetre bumps across the upper trunk that itch when you sweat.

## How to spot it

- Every bump has a hair in the middle.
- It itches.
- It appeared after shaving, sweating, a hot tub or a course of antibiotics.
- Acne creams have not helped.

## Causes and triggers

Bacteria (usually Staphylococcus), Malassezia yeast, friction from tight clothing, occlusion by oils, and regrowing hairs after shaving ([razor bumps in the beard area](/conditions/razor-bumps/), [ingrown hairs](/conditions/ingrown-hairs/)). Lagos heat and humidity favour all of them, and steroid or lightening creams make them worse.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - bumps persist beyond two to three weeks or keep returning
> - they are spreading, painful or forming boils
> - you have many identical itchy bumps on the chest and back
> - marks or scars are forming

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Squeezing
> - "Triple action" and steroid creams
> - Heavy oils and butters on the chest and back
> - Sharing razors and towels

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

Examination with a dermatoscope, a swab from a pustule, and a scraping examined for yeast ([fungal tests](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/fungal-tests/)) identify the type.

## Folliculitis treatment at the clinic

Bacterial folliculitis is treated with antiseptic washes and antibiotic lotion or tablets. Yeast folliculitis needs antifungal treatment and often maintenance in hot months. For shaving-related cases, technique is changed and [laser hair reduction](/services/laser-treatments/laser-hair-removal/) considered. Dark marks are treated once new bumps stop.

## Prevention and daily care

- Shower and change soon after sweating.
- Wear loose, breathable fabrics.
- Shave with the grain using a clean blade, or trim instead.
