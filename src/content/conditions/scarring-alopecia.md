## What scarring alopecia looks like on African hair

In CCCA, the crown thins first, often noticed by a hairdresser. The centre becomes smooth and slightly shiny, and the follicle openings, normally seen as tiny dots, disappear. Around the edge, hairs are sparse and may break easily. A magnifier shows pale halos around surviving hairs. The scalp may look entirely calm despite active disease.

Other types include discoid [lupus](/conditions/cutaneous-lupus/) (scaly patches with dark rims and pale centres), lichen planopilaris (scale and darkening around individual hairs with tenderness), frontal fibrosing alopecia (a band of loss along the front hairline with thinning eyebrows), and folliculitis decalvans (pus spots and tufts).

## How to spot it

- Loss is centred on the crown or along the front hairline, and slowly enlarging.
- The skin in the area is smooth, with no pores.
- The scalp itches, burns or is tender, even slightly.
- A mother, sister or aunt has similar loss.

Loss confined to the temples with fine fringe hairs is more often [traction alopecia](/conditions/traction-alopecia/); a widening parting with visible pores suggests [female pattern hair loss](/conditions/female-pattern-hair-loss/). These can coexist.

## Causes and triggers

Immune cells attack the upper part of the follicle, where its stem cells and oil gland lie. Once those are destroyed, the follicle cannot regenerate.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist soon if
> - your crown is thinning or tender
> - hair is breaking at the crown
> - your front hairline is moving back and your eyebrows are thinning
> - there are bald areas with smooth or shiny skin

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Covering a thinning crown with glued or tightly sewn weaves
> - Relaxers, hot combs and high heat
> - Hair oils and growth pomades in place of a diagnosis
> - Waiting to see if it recovers by itself

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

[Trichoscopy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/trichoscopy/) identifies loss of follicle openings and signs of inflammation. A small scalp [biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) from the active edge confirms the type. Photographs and measurements record the area for comparison.

## Scarring alopecia treatment at the clinic

- **Stop inflammation:** potent steroid lotion, steroid injections into the edge every four to eight weeks, and a course of anti-inflammatory tablets.
- **Support surviving hair:** topical minoxidil.
- **Protect:** loose, low-tension styles; minimal heat and chemicals.
- **Monitor:** photographs every three to six months. Treatment continues until the disease has been quiet for at least a year.
- **Restore:** [hair transplant](/services/hair-and-scalp/hair-transplant/) may be considered only after prolonged inactivity, with a lower success rate than in non-scarring loss; hairpieces that do not pull are a good alternative.

## Prevention and daily care

- Have any persistent scalp tenderness, itching or crown thinning examined.
- Ask relatives with crown hair loss to be checked.
- Keep hairstyles loose and scalp care gentle.
