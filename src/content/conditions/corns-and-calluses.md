## What corns and calluses look like on brown and Black skin

Thickened skin appears yellow-grey, waxy or darker brown than the surrounding foot. Hard corns form on the tops and tips of the toes and under the ball of the foot; a translucent core is visible at the centre. Soft corns between the fourth and fifth toes are white and rubbery from moisture. Calluses spread across the heel, the ball of the foot and the outer edge of the big toe. On the hands they match the grip of a tool, pen or weight bar.

## How to spot it

- It lies exactly where a shoe rubs or weight falls.
- It is painful to pressure, painless at rest.
- The other foot often has a matching one.

Black dots and interrupted skin lines indicate a [plantar wart](/conditions/plantar-warts/).

## Causes and triggers

Skin thickens to protect itself from repeated friction. Narrow or stiff shoes, thin-soled sandals on hard ground, toe deformities and long hours on the feet concentrate that friction.

> [!DERM] See a doctor if
> - you have diabetes, numb feet or poor circulation and any corn or callus
> - a corn is very painful, bleeding or discharging
> - there is a dark spot within a callus
> - it has not improved after a month of better footwear

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Blades, scissors and metal graters
> - Acid corn plasters if you have diabetes or fragile skin
> - Shoes that pinch the toes

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The doctor pares the surface to distinguish corn, callus and wart, and examines foot shape, gait and footwear. People with diabetes have sensation and circulation checked.

## Treatment at the clinic

Painless paring gives immediate relief. A urea or salicylic acid preparation softens what remains. Pads, toe separators and insoles redistribute pressure, and referral for orthotic or surgical advice is made for fixed deformities. See [foot skin care](/services/foot-skin-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Choose shoes with a wide toe box and cushioned sole; shop in the afternoon when feet are largest.
- Moisturise feet nightly and file thick skin lightly once a week.
- Wear socks that fit.
