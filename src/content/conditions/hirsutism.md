## What hirsutism looks like on brown and Black skin

The hairs are thick, dark and curly, unlike the fine, soft hair that covers most of the face and body. On the chin and neck, repeated plucking, threading or shaving of curly hair produces [ingrown hairs](/conditions/ingrown-hairs/), bumps and a dense scatter of dark marks, so many women consult about "pimples" or "dark chin" before mentioning the hair.

Related signs help point to the cause: irregular periods, acne along the jaw, thinning at the crown ([female pattern hair loss](/conditions/female-pattern-hair-loss/)) and dark, velvety skin at the neck ([acanthosis nigricans](/conditions/dark-underarms-inner-thighs-knees-and-neck/)).

## How to spot it

- Hairs are coarse and pigmented, not fine down.
- They are in areas where men typically grow hair.
- You remove hair from the face weekly or more.
- Periods are irregular, or you have acne or weight gain around the waist.

## Causes and triggers

- **Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)**
- **Idiopathic hirsutism:** normal hormones and periods, sensitive follicles
- **Medicines and supplements,** including body-building steroids
- **Other hormonal conditions,** which are uncommon
- **Topical steroid misuse** on the face, which causes fine hair growth

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - coarse facial or body hair is bothering you
> - hair growth is rapid or began suddenly
> - your voice has deepened or periods have stopped
> - hair removal is leaving bumps, scars or dark marks

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Plucking and threading coarse curly hairs on the chin
> - Hair removal creams left on longer than directed
> - Lightening creams on the chin and neck
> - Herbal "hormone balancing" products of unknown content

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The dermatologist scores the hair pattern, examines the skin and scalp, and takes a menstrual and medication history. Hormone and metabolic tests are arranged with the [internal medicine service](/services/internal-medicine/general-medical-consultation/), with referral to a gynaecologist or endocrinologist when needed.

## Hirsutism treatment at the clinic

- **Treat the cause:** weight management, and hormonal tablets where appropriate. These slow new growth over six to twelve months and require reliable contraception with some medicines.
- **[Laser hair removal](/services/laser-treatments/laser-hair-removal/)** using a long-wavelength laser that is safer for dark skin, in a course of sessions. This also reduces ingrown hairs.
- **A prescription cream** that slows facial hair growth while it is used.
- **Skin repair:** treatment of bumps and [dark marks](/conditions/hyperpigmentation/).

The service is part of [men's and women's skin health](/services/mens-and-womens-skin-health/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Trim or shave with the grain between laser sessions; do not pluck.
- Use sunscreen on treated and marked areas.
- Keep active and maintain a healthy weight.
