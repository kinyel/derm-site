## What a fungal nail looks like

The commonest pattern starts at the free edge or one side of a big toenail: a white-yellow or brown streak that spreads towards the cuticle, with chalky debris building up underneath and the nail thickening until it is hard to cut. Other patterns are white, powdery patches on the nail surface, and, in people with weak immunity, change that begins at the cuticle.

In people with dark skin, brown bands running the length of several nails are usually normal pigment. A single dark band that is widening needs a check; see [moles and when to worry](/conditions/moles/).

Fingernail infection is often caused by yeast (Candida) in people whose hands are frequently wet, and comes with a swollen, tender nail fold ([paronychia](/conditions/paronychia/)).

## How to spot it

- One or two toenails are affected, not all twenty at once.
- The change began at the tip or edge and moved backwards.
- There is peeling or itching between the toes or dry scaling on the soles.
- The nail is crumbly, not just ridged.

Pits, salmon-coloured patches and many nails involved together suggest [nail psoriasis](/conditions/nail-psoriasis/).

## Causes and triggers

Dermatophyte fungi that cause [athlete's foot](/conditions/athletes-foot/) invade the nail from the surrounding skin. Warm, damp shoes, repeated small injuries and reduced blood supply make the nail easier to infect. Lagos humidity keeps feet moist for much of the year.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - a nail is thick, discoloured or lifting and you want it treated
> - you have diabetes or poor circulation and any nail or foot change
> - the skin around a nail is red, swollen or painful
> - a dark streak appears in one nail
> - pharmacy treatment has not helped after six months

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Tablets bought without a test or medical review
> - Bleach, battery fluid or acid on the nail
> - Pulling off a loose nail yourself
> - Sharing clippers, files and shoes

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

Clippings and scrapings from under the nail are sent for microscopy and culture ([fungal tests](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/fungal-tests/)). Microscopy results return within days; culture takes several weeks and identifies the organism.

## Nail fungus treatment at the clinic

- **Tablets** give the highest cure rates and are preferred when several nails or the nail root are involved.
- **Lacquer or solution** painted on for six to twelve months suits mild infection of the outer part of one or two nails, and people who cannot take tablets.
- **Thinning** of thick nail helps medicine penetrate and makes shoes comfortable.
- **Skin treatment** for athlete's foot, and advice for the household.
- **Removal** of a painful, badly damaged nail is occasionally advised.

Cure is judged by healthy nail growing in, and repeat testing if needed. See [nail care](/services/nail-care/) for the full service.

## Prevention and daily care

- Dry between the toes after bathing.
- Wear open footwear when you can; rotate closed shoes.
- Treat athlete's foot early.
- Cut nails straight across and keep them short.
- Discard or disinfect old shoes after treatment.
