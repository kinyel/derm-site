## What nail psoriasis looks like

Changes depend on which part of the nail unit is inflamed. Disease in the nail root produces pits, ridges and crumbling. Disease in the nail bed produces lifting with a darker border, discoloured patches like a drop of oil under the nail, thick chalky build-up beneath the tip and tiny dark splinter lines. On dark skin, the surrounding nail fold may be darker and scaly, and normal brown nail bands can coexist.

## How to spot it

- More than three nails are affected.
- You can see pits when light falls across the nail.
- There is scaling skin elsewhere, or dandruff that never clears.
- Antifungal treatment has failed.

## Causes and triggers

The immune overactivity of [psoriasis](/conditions/psoriasis/) speeds up and disrupts nail formation. Injury to the nail, including aggressive manicures, picking and tight shoes, triggers flares (the Koebner response).

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - several nails are changing
> - nails are painful or limit use of your hands
> - you have joint pain, stiffness or swelling
> - a single nail has a dark streak or a growth, which needs separate assessment

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Antifungal tablets without a positive test
> - Cleaning under the nail with sharp tools
> - Acrylic nails, gel removal by scraping, and cuticle cutting
> - Biting and picking

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The dermatologist examines all twenty nails, the skin and scalp, and asks about joints. A clipping is sent to exclude fungus ([fungal tests](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/fungal-tests/)). A nail biopsy is rarely needed.

## Nail psoriasis treatment at the clinic

Mild disease: a steroid and vitamin D preparation applied to the nail folds and under the tip for several months. Stubborn nails: small steroid injections beside the nail. Severe disease or arthritis: tablets or biologic injections, with rheumatology input. Nails grow slowly, so improvement is judged over six to twelve months. See [nail care](/services/nail-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Keep nails short and dry; wear gloves for wet work.
- Moisturise nails and cuticles daily.
- Choose shoes with room for the toes.
