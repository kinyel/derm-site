## What head lice look like

Adult lice are grey-brown, about the size of a sesame seed, and move quickly away from light. They are hard to see on a dark scalp; a bright torch and a fine comb help. Nits are easier to find: pale or tan specks fixed to hairs within a centimetre of the scalp, mostly behind the ears and at the nape. Empty egg cases further along the hair show past, not current, infestation.

## How to spot it

- Part the hair in sections under good light and look at the nape and behind the ears.
- Comb conditioner-coated wet hair with a lice comb and wipe it on white tissue.
- Only a live, moving louse confirms active infestation.

Itching with greasy flakes and no nits points to [dandruff](/conditions/seborrhoeic-dermatitis-and-dandruff/).

## Causes and triggers

Lice crawl from one head to another during close contact; they cannot jump or fly, and they survive less than two days off the scalp. Outbreaks follow school terms and sleepovers.

> [!DERM] See a doctor if
> - lice persist after two properly applied treatments
> - the scalp has sores, crusts or pus
> - you are unsure whether specks are nits
> - a baby under six months is affected

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Kerosene, petrol or insecticide spray on the scalp: these are flammable and poisonous
> - Repeating lice lotion more often than directed
> - Treating "just in case" when no live lice are found

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The doctor examines the scalp with a magnifier ([trichoscopy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/trichoscopy/)) to confirm live lice or viable eggs and to exclude other causes of scalp itch.

## Head lice treatment

- Apply a recommended lice lotion to dry hair and scalp for the stated time, then rinse. Repeat after seven days to kill newly hatched lice.
- Or wet-comb every three to four days for two weeks.
- Check everyone in the home and treat only those with live lice, on the same day.
- Wash combs and brushes in hot water; bedding and hats can be washed or left unused for two days.

Children are seen within [paediatric dermatology](/services/childrens-skin-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Check children's hair weekly during school term.
- Tie long hair back for school.
- Do not share combs, hats or hair ties.
