## What breakage looks like on African hair

Tightly coiled hair has a flattened shape and a twist at every turn, and natural scalp oil does not travel easily down the coil. Each bend is a weak point. When the outer layer (cuticle) is stripped by chemicals or heat, the inner fibres fray like a rope and the hair snaps, leaving a brush-like tip.

Breakage shows as patches of short, rough hair among longer hair, thin ends that you can see through, and length that never seems to increase. The scalp looks normal and follicles are intact, which separates it from true hair loss.

## How to spot it

- Slide a hair between your fingers: do you feel small bumps or see white specks?
- Are broken pieces shorter than your full hair length?
- Is the damage worst where you apply heat or relaxer, or where a band rubs?
- Did it follow a new chemical service by a few weeks?

## Causes and triggers

- **Chemicals:** relaxers, texturisers, perms, bleach and permanent dyes.
- **Heat:** straighteners and hot combs, especially on damp or already relaxed hair.
- **Mechanical stress:** tight elastic, fine-tooth combs, brushing dry coils, and frequent restyling.
- **Dryness:** Lagos sun, sea water, chlorinated pools and, in the dry season, harmattan air.
- **Scratching** an itchy scalp from [dandruff](/conditions/seborrhoeic-dermatitis-and-dandruff/) or product reactions.
- **Health factors:** low iron, crash diets and thyroid disease make hair more brittle.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - breakage continues three months after you changed your routine
> - the scalp is itchy, sore, scaly or has bumps
> - you had a chemical burn from a relaxer or dye
> - there are bare or shiny areas, not just short hair

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Relaxing more often than every eight to ten weeks, or overlapping onto relaxed lengths
> - Colour and relaxer within the same fortnight
> - Daily direct heat
> - Combing from the roots down; start at the ends
> - Products promising to "repair" hair permanently

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The dermatologist examines hair shafts and scalp with [trichoscopy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/trichoscopy/) to confirm breakage, look for scalp disease, and rule out early scarring. Blood tests are arranged if your history suggests a deficiency or thyroid problem.

## Hair breakage treatment at the clinic

There is no medicine for the hair shaft; care is a plan:

1. **Pause** chemical and heat services for a period agreed with you.
2. **Trim** frayed ends in stages.
3. **Wash and condition** every one to two weeks with a gentle shampoo and a conditioner left on for a few minutes; detangle wet with a wide-tooth comb.
4. **Seal in moisture** with a light leave-in product.
5. **Choose low-tension styles** and sleep on satin.
6. **Treat the scalp** if dandruff, dermatitis or folliculitis is present.

This advice is part of every [hair and scalp care](/services/hair-and-scalp/) consultation.

## Prevention and daily care

- Stretch the interval between relaxers, or grow out natural hair.
- Use a heat protectant and the lowest effective temperature.
- Wear a swim cap and rinse hair after swimming.
- Eat regular meals with protein, iron and vegetables.
