## What acne scars look like on brown and Black skin

Scars and pigment usually appear together on dark skin, and the pigment draws the eye first. A cheek can look heavily scarred when most of what is visible is flat brown marks around a few shallow dents. Stretch the skin gently between two fingers: marks stay the same, shallow scars flatten and deep ones remain.

The types:

- **Ice-pick scars:** narrow, deep holes, like large pores, mostly on the cheeks.
- **Boxcar scars:** round or oval dents with defined edges.
- **Rolling scars:** broad, shallow dips that give the skin a wavy look, most obvious in side lighting.
- **Hypertrophic scars and [keloids](/conditions/keloids/):** raised, firm scars on the jawline, chest, shoulders and upper back. These are more common in people of African descent.
- **Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation:** flat dark marks; see [hyperpigmentation](/conditions/hyperpigmentation/).

## How to spot it

- Dents or pits that remain months after the spots have gone
- Uneven texture that catches the light
- Firm raised lumps where acne was severe
- Dark marks that are flat to the touch (not scars, but treated alongside)

## Causes and triggers

A scar forms when inflammation in a deep spot destroys collagen and the skin repairs itself imperfectly. Too little new collagen leaves a dent; too much leaves a raised scar. The risk rises with:

- nodules and cysts
- acne that goes untreated for years
- squeezing, picking and rough extraction
- a family tendency to scarring
- inflamed acne on the chest, shoulders and jaw in people prone to keloids

The single most effective way to prevent scars is to treat [acne](/conditions/acne/) early.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - new dents or raised scars are appearing: your acne needs stronger treatment now
> - scars or marks affect your confidence
> - a scar is growing, itching or spreading beyond the original spot
> - you have had a peel, needling or laser elsewhere and the skin darkened

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Starting scar procedures while acne is still active
> - Home dermarollers with long needles and peel acids bought online
> - Aggressive laser or deep peels offered without a test patch
> - Bleaching creams to "even out" scars
> - Sun exposure during a course of treatment

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The dermatologist examines your skin in angled light, maps the scar types and checks that acne is controlled. Your tendency to pigment and to form keloids is noted, because it changes what can safely be offered. Photographs are taken, with your consent, so that progress can be judged fairly.

## Acne scar treatment: options at the clinic

No one method treats every scar. A plan usually layers several:

| Scar type | Treatments used |
|---|---|
| Rolling | Subcision; [microneedling](/services/aesthetic-treatments/microneedling/); radiofrequency microneedling |
| Boxcar | Microneedling; fractional laser; punch techniques for deep ones |
| Ice-pick | TCA CROSS (a strong peel placed in each pit); punch excision |
| Raised scars and keloids | Steroid injections; silicone; pressure |
| Dark marks | Sunscreen; pigment creams; [superficial peels](/services/aesthetic-treatments/chemical-peels/) |

**Subcision** releases the fibrous bands that pull rolling scars down, using a needle under local anaesthetic. **TCA CROSS** places a tiny drop of peel solution in each ice-pick scar to make it fill with new collagen. **[PRP](/services/aesthetic-treatments/prp-microneedling/)** may be added to needling. **[Fractional laser resurfacing](/services/laser-treatments/laser-skin-resurfacing/)** and fractional [picosecond laser](/services/laser-treatments/pico-laser/) are options when pigment risk has been assessed and a test area has healed well. A small amount of **filler** can lift an individual depressed scar. Surgical [scar revision](/services/skin-surgery/scar-revision/) is occasionally used.

On dark skin the order matters: the skin is prepared with sunscreen and pigment-calming cream, the gentlest effective method is used first, and treatments are spaced so that the skin recovers fully.

## Prevention and daily care

- Keep acne under control; continue maintenance treatment after it clears.
- Do not pick or squeeze.
- Wear sunscreen every day during and after scar treatment.
- Use the retinoid your dermatologist prescribes; it supports collagen and fades marks.
- Be patient: collagen keeps improving for months after the last treatment.
