## What it looks like in Black men

The pattern is the same in every population: the temples recede, the crown thins, and eventually the two areas may meet, leaving a horseshoe of hair at the back and sides. Many Nigerian men keep their hair cut low, so the first sign is often a hairline that the barber can no longer shape, or a shiny crown noticed in a photograph.

Two other conditions are common in the same men and are sometimes confused with balding: bumps and scars at the back of the head after close haircuts ([scalp folliculitis](/conditions/scalp-folliculitis/) and acne keloidalis), and round smooth patches of [alopecia areata](/conditions/alopecia-areata/).

## How to spot it

- The loss is at the temples and crown, not in round patches.
- It has progressed slowly over years.
- Your father, grandfathers or uncles have a similar pattern.
- The scalp is healthy, with no scale, pain or bumps.

## Causes and triggers

An enzyme in the scalp converts testosterone to DHT. In men who inherit sensitive follicles, DHT shortens the growth phase and shrinks the follicle a little more with each cycle. Testosterone injections and anabolic steroids used for body-building accelerate the process.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - you want to slow or treat your hair loss
> - loss is sudden, patchy or accompanied by itching, pain or scale
> - you are considering a hair transplant
> - you are buying hair loss tablets online without a medical review

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Unlicensed tablets and "hair growth" injections
> - Transplant offers that skip a medical assessment
> - Stopping proven treatment after a few weeks because of early shedding
> - Anabolic steroids

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

Examination and [trichoscopy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/trichoscopy/) confirm the pattern and show how many follicles are still active. Tests are seldom needed.

## Male pattern hair loss treatment at the clinic

- **Topical minoxidil** applied daily to the scalp.
- **Finasteride,** a tablet that lowers DHT in the scalp, prescribed after discussing benefits and side effects.
- **Combination treatment,** which works better than either alone.
- **[PRP for hair loss](/services/hair-and-scalp/prp-for-hair-loss/)** as an add-on for some men.
- **[Hair transplant](/services/hair-and-scalp/hair-transplant/)** for stable loss with good donor hair. Medicine is usually continued to protect the remaining hair.
- **No treatment** is a perfectly reasonable choice; the condition is harmless.

Results are judged at six and twelve months with photographs, within the [hair and scalp](/services/hair-and-scalp/) service.

## Prevention and daily care

- Begin treatment early if you intend to treat at all.
- Use sunscreen or a cap on a thinning crown.
- Ask your barber to disinfect clippers.
