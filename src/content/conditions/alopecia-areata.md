## What alopecia areata looks like on brown and Black skin

A coin-sized patch of completely bare scalp appears, often found by a barber or hairdresser. The skin is smooth, the same colour as the surrounding scalp, with no scale, scarring or soreness. At the edges, a magnifier shows short "exclamation mark" hairs that are narrower at the base. Patches may multiply or merge. In men, the beard is a common site.

Less often, all scalp hair is lost (alopecia totalis) or all body hair (alopecia universalis). When hair returns it may be fine and white at first before regaining its colour and curl. Nails can show rows of pinpoint pits.

## How to spot it

- The patch appeared quickly, over days to weeks.
- The skin in it is smooth, not scaly or shiny-scarred.
- There is no pain or itch, or only mild tingling.
- Hair at the edge comes out with a gentle pull while the patch is active.

Scaly patches with broken hairs, especially in a child, need a [fungal test](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/fungal-tests/). Loss confined to the hairline suggests [traction alopecia](/conditions/traction-alopecia/).

## Causes and triggers

Immune cells gather around the growing hair bulb and switch it off. The follicle stays alive and can restart. The tendency runs in families and is linked with thyroid disease, [vitiligo](/conditions/vitiligo/) and atopic [eczema](/conditions/eczema/).

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - you find a bald patch on the scalp, beard or eyebrows
> - patches are spreading or joining
> - a child has patchy hair loss
> - hair loss is affecting your mood, work or school

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Onion, garlic, pepper or battery fluid rubbed into patches; these burn the scalp
> - Unlabelled "alopecia creams"
> - Tight styles to cover patches, which add traction loss
> - Long courses of steroid tablets without specialist supervision

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The diagnosis is usually made on examination with [trichoscopy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/trichoscopy/). Thyroid tests may be arranged. A [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) is reserved for unclear cases.

## Alopecia areata treatment at the clinic

- **Limited patches:** steroid injections into the patch, or a potent steroid lotion for children and for those who prefer no needles. Watching without treatment is reasonable for a single small patch.
- **Extensive or persistent loss:** contact immunotherapy or tablets that calm the immune system. JAK inhibitor tablets are a newer option for severe disease, prescribed after screening tests and with monitoring.
- **Support:** wigs and hairpieces that do not pull, eyebrow options, and honest discussion of what to expect. The emotional effect is taken as seriously as the hair.

Care is provided within the [hair and scalp](/services/hair-and-scalp/) service; children are seen with a parent within [paediatric dermatology](/services/childrens-skin-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Protect bare scalp from sun with a hat or sunscreen.
- Keep hairstyles loose over and around patches.
- Photograph patches monthly to track change.
- Read [Will my hair grow back?](/blog/will-my-hair-grow-back/) for how regrowth differs between types of hair loss.
