## What ingrown hairs look like on brown and Black skin

Each one is a small bump, skin-coloured to dark brown, sometimes with a yellow head, and often with a visible hair coiled beneath the surface. On dark skin the lasting problem is the stain: weeks of hair removal leave a peppering of dark dots along the bikini line, underarms, legs or chin that many people find more troubling than the bumps.

Common sites:

- **Bikini line and pubic area,** where hair is coarse and curly and underwear rubs
- **Underarms**
- **Lower legs and thighs**
- **Chin and neck in women** who pluck or thread; persistent coarse hair here may point to [hirsutism](/conditions/hirsutism/)
- **Beard area in men;** see [razor bumps](/conditions/razor-bumps/)

## How to spot it

- Bumps appear only where you remove hair.
- They follow hair removal by a day or more.
- A hair can be seen in or beside the bump.
- They clear if you let the hair grow.

## Causes and triggers

Curly hair cut to a sharp point re-enters the skin as it grows; hair pulled out by wax or tweezers regrows through a narrow channel that dead skin cells can block. Tight clothing presses hairs back against the skin, and heat and sweat add bacteria.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - bumps are frequent, painful or infected
> - you get deep lumps or abscesses in the groin or armpits
> - dark marks are building up
> - you are a woman with coarse hair on the chin, chest or abdomen

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Digging with pins, needles or blades
> - Shaving over inflamed bumps
> - Bleaching creams on the dark marks, especially in the groin
> - Scrubbing with rough sponges
> - Waxing or threading over broken skin

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

A look with a dermatoscope shows the trapped hairs. A swab is taken from infected bumps, and women with excess hair growth may be offered hormone tests through the [internal medicine service](/services/internal-medicine/general-medical-consultation/).

## Ingrown hair treatment at the clinic

- **Hair removal advice.** Trimming with clippers causes the fewest ingrown hairs. For a shave: soften the hair, use gel, shave with the grain using a sharp single blade, and do not stretch the skin.
- **Creams.** A retinoid or a mild acid lotion keeps follicle openings clear; an antibiotic lotion treats infected bumps.
- **Release.** A trapped hair is freed with a sterile needle.
- **[Laser hair removal](/services/laser-treatments/laser-hair-removal/).** A long-wavelength laser suited to dark skin reduces the hair itself and is the most lasting answer for the bikini line, underarms and chin.
- **Marks.** Dark spots are treated as described under [hyperpigmentation](/conditions/hyperpigmentation/), sometimes with [chemical peels](/services/aesthetic-treatments/chemical-peels/).

These services sit within [men's and women's skin health](/services/mens-and-womens-skin-health/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Exfoliate gently two or three times a week; see [How to exfoliate your skin safely](/blog/how-to-exfoliate-your-skin-safely/).
- Moisturise after hair removal.
- Wear cotton underwear that does not rub the bikini line.
- Replace blades often and never share them.
