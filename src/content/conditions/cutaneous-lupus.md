## What cutaneous lupus looks like on brown and Black skin

Discoid patches start as raised, scaly, dark violet or brown plaques. As they mature, the centre sinks, loses pigment and scars, while a dark rim spreads outward, giving a striking pale-centre, dark-border appearance. The outer ear and its inner bowl are typical sites, with pitted scars. On the scalp, patches destroy hair follicles. The lips can be involved.

The acute "butterfly" rash of systemic lupus appears on dark skin as a dusky, slightly raised darkening over the cheeks and bridge of the nose that spares the folds beside the nostrils.

## How to spot it

- Patches worsen after sun.
- They leave scars and light or dark marks.
- The ears or scalp are involved.
- Lifting a scale shows small spikes on its underside.

## Causes and triggers

Genes, hormones and ultraviolet light together set off autoimmunity. Smoking makes skin lupus harder to control and blunts the effect of treatment.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist promptly if
> - you have scaly patches on the face, ears or scalp that scar
> - you are losing hair in patches with scalp changes
> - a facial rash follows sun exposure
> - you also have joint pain, mouth ulcers, fever, chest pain or swollen ankles

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Sun exposure without protection
> - Smoking
> - Lightening or mixed creams on the patches
> - Delaying treatment of scalp patches

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

A [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) confirms the diagnosis. Blood and urine tests look for systemic lupus, arranged with the [internal medicine service](/services/internal-medicine/general-medical-consultation/). An eye check is organised before and during antimalarial treatment.

## Treatment at the clinic

Sun protection, a potent steroid ointment or injections for active patches, and hydroxychloroquine tablets for widespread or scarring disease. Other tablets are used if needed. Pigment change and scars are addressed once the disease is quiet, with camouflage advice meanwhile.

## Prevention and daily care

- SPF 50 broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning, reapplied outdoors.
- Wide-brimmed hat and long sleeves.
- Do not smoke.
- Report new symptoms early.
