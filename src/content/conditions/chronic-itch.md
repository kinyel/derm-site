## What chronic itch looks like on brown and Black skin

The skin may look normal, or show only the results of scratching: lines of broken skin, shiny nails, and patches that have become thick, dark and leathery with exaggerated skin lines (lichen simplex). In prurigo nodularis, dozens of firm, dark brown bumps develop on the forearms, shins and upper back, sparing the middle of the back where the hands cannot reach. All of these heal with marked darkening.

## How to spot it

- Itching has gone on for more than six weeks.
- It disturbs sleep.
- Moisturiser and antihistamines have made little difference.
- You may have other symptoms: tiredness, thirst, weight change, yellow eyes.

## Causes and triggers

Causes fall into four groups: skin disease ([eczema](/conditions/eczema/), [scabies](/conditions/scabies/), fungal infection, [lichen planus](/conditions/lichen-planus/)); internal disease; nerve-related itch in a single area; and medicines. In older adults, simple dryness is frequent. Heat, sweat and hot baths lower the itch threshold.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - itching has lasted more than two weeks without explanation
> - you have night sweats, fever, weight loss or yellowing of the eyes
> - the skin is thickening or forming bumps from scratching
> - sleep or mood is affected

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Hot water, medicated soaps and antiseptic baths
> - Long-term use of steroid creams without diagnosis
> - Scratching with combs, brushes or sponges

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

A full skin examination looks for subtle signs of scabies and other rashes. Blood tests cover blood count, iron, kidney, liver and thyroid function and glucose, arranged with the [internal medicine service](/services/internal-medicine/general-medical-consultation/). A [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) is taken if a rash is present but unclear.

## Chronic itch treatment at the clinic

- Correct the cause where one is found.
- Restore the skin barrier with soap substitutes and thick moisturisers.
- Calm scratched areas with prescribed creams, sometimes under a dressing.
- Use tablets that reduce itch signalling when itch is severe.
- Offer [phototherapy](/services/clinical-dermatology/phototherapy/) for widespread itch.

## Prevention and daily care

- Keep baths short and lukewarm; moisturise straight after.
- Keep nails short; press or cool the skin when it itches.
- Wear loose cotton and keep the bedroom cool.
