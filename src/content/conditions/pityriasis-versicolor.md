## What pityriasis versicolor looks like on brown and Black skin

On dark skin the patches are usually lighter than normal: tan, beige or pale brown spots a few millimetres to a few centimetres across, scattered over the upper chest, back, shoulders, neck and upper arms. They can also be darker than the surrounding skin, or pinkish, which is where "versicolor" (changing colour) comes from. One person may have both.

In Nigeria it commonly also affects the face, especially the forehead, hairline and sides of the face in children and teenagers. The surface carries a very fine scale that becomes visible when you scratch the patch lightly with a fingernail.

## How to spot it

- Many small patches, not one large one
- Concentrated on the upper trunk and neck
- A dusty scale appears when scratched
- Worse in the hot season or after a period of heavy sweating
- Little or no itch

A single, completely white, smooth patch is more likely to be [vitiligo](/conditions/vitiligo/). A ring with a raised, scaly edge and clear centre suggests [ringworm](/conditions/ringworm-and-fungal-skin-infections/). Our article [What is this white patch on my skin?](/blog/what-is-this-white-patch-on-my-skin/) compares the common causes.

## Causes and triggers

The yeast Malassezia feeds on skin oil. In heat and humidity it changes into a form that grows into the outer skin layer and interferes with pigment. Triggers include sweating in tight or synthetic clothing, applying oils, petroleum jelly or thick creams to the chest and back, and steroid creams. It is not caused by poor hygiene.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - pharmacy treatment has not cleared the scale within four weeks
> - the rash covers a large area or keeps returning
> - patches are completely white, numb or have no scale
> - you have used a mixed steroid cream on it

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - "Triple action" and steroid creams: they feed the yeast and thin the skin
> - Lightening creams to "even out" the colour
> - Scrubbing with harsh sponges, bleach or antiseptic liquid
> - Heavy oils on the chest and back

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The appearance is usually enough. A Wood's lamp may show a yellow-green glow, and a [skin scraping](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/fungal-tests/) examined under the microscope confirms the yeast within minutes when there is doubt. A pale patch with reduced sensation is assessed for other causes.

## Pityriasis versicolor treatment at the clinic

- **Antifungal shampoo as a body wash.** Applied to the affected skin and scalp, left on for the advised number of minutes and rinsed, daily for the prescribed period.
- **Antifungal cream** for a few small patches.
- **Antifungal tablets** for widespread or stubborn rash, after checking your other medicines.
- **Maintenance.** A wash once or twice a month through the hottest, most humid months to prevent relapse.

Expect the scale to go first and the colour to follow slowly. Treatment is part of [clinical dermatology](/services/clinical-dermatology/) at the clinic, and follow-up can be done by [online consultation](/services/dermatology-consultations/online-consultation/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Shower soon after exercise and change out of damp clothes.
- Wear loose cotton or moisture-wicking fabrics.
- Keep oils and thick creams off the chest and back.
- Use the maintenance wash as advised.
