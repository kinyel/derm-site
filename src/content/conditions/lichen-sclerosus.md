## What lichen sclerosus looks like on brown and Black skin

The affected skin loses colour and becomes ivory-white, thin and wrinkled like cigarette paper, in sharp contrast with normally dark genital skin. In women it often forms a figure-of-eight around the vulva and anus. Small purple bruises, splits and erosions appear with friction. Over time the inner lips can flatten and fuse and the clitoral hood can seal over. In men the foreskin develops a tight white ring and becomes hard to retract.

The white colour is sometimes confused with [vitiligo (loss of pigment)](/conditions/vitiligo/), which is not itchy and does not alter the skin's texture.

## How to spot it

- Persistent itch or soreness of genital skin.
- White, fragile skin that tears easily.
- No lasting improvement with thrush treatment.

## Causes and triggers

The cause is not fully understood; autoimmunity and genetic tendency contribute. Friction and contact with urine can aggravate it.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - you have ongoing genital itching, soreness or white patches
> - sex, passing urine or opening the bowels is painful
> - the foreskin has become tight
> - a child has genital itching, soreness or bruising
> - a sore or lump does not heal

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Repeated antifungal or mixed creams without a diagnosis
> - Soap, wipes, douches and fragranced products on genital skin
> - Tight synthetic underwear
> - Stopping treatment as soon as itching eases

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The appearance is often diagnostic. A small [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) under local anaesthetic confirms it in adults when needed. Examination is private, with a chaperone offered. The clinic is led by a woman; see [the lead dermatologist's profile]({{leadPath}}).

## Lichen sclerosus treatment at the clinic

A potent steroid ointment is applied daily at first and then stepped down to a maintenance schedule, with clear guidance on how much to use. A soap substitute and barrier ointment protect the skin. Reviews are arranged at intervals. Urology or gynaecology colleagues are involved for scarring. Care is confidential within [men's and women's skin health](/services/mens-and-womens-skin-health/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Wash with water or a soap substitute; pat dry.
- Apply a barrier ointment before and after passing urine if sore.
- Examine the area monthly and report changes.
