## What impetigo looks like on brown and Black skin

It starts as a small blister or sore that breaks quickly, oozes and dries to a stuck-on golden or dark honey crust. The surrounding skin is slightly darker than normal; redness is subtle. New sores appear nearby as the child scratches. The blistering form produces larger, floppy blisters on the trunk, nappy area or limbs that rupture to leave round, shiny patches with a rim of scale. Healed areas are paler or darker for a while.

## How to spot it

- Crusts are yellow-brown and moist beneath.
- Sores are multiplying over days.
- Siblings or classmates have the same.

Grouped tiny blisters at the lip edge that recur in the same place are more likely [cold sores](/conditions/cold-sores/).

## Causes and triggers

Staphylococcus aureus or Streptococcus bacteria enter through broken skin: a scratch, bite, [eczema](/conditions/eczema/) or [scabies](/conditions/scabies/) burrow. Heat and humidity favour it.

> [!DERM] See a doctor if
> - sores are spreading or have not improved after three days of treatment
> - the child has a fever or is unwell
> - there is swelling of the face or dark, cola-coloured urine in the following weeks
> - infections keep returning

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Steroid or mixed creams on the sores
> - Picking crusts
> - Sharing towels, bedding and clothing
> - Leftover antibiotics

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

Diagnosis is by examination. A swab is taken when infection is extensive, recurrent or not responding, to choose the right antibiotic.

## Impetigo treatment at the clinic

A few sores are treated with a prescribed antiseptic or antibiotic cream after soaking off the crusts with warm water. Many sores, blisters or fever call for antibiotic tablets or syrup for the full course. The underlying itch is treated at the same visit. Children are seen within [children's skin care](/services/childrens-skin-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Wash hands often and keep nails short.
- Clean cuts and bites and cover them.
- Wash towels and bedding in hot water during treatment.
