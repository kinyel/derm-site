## What an ingrown toenail looks like

In the first stage the skin beside the nail is swollen, tender and slightly darker or redder than usual. In the second, it weeps clear or yellow fluid and may smell. In the third, a soft, moist, fleshy lump of healing tissue grows over the nail edge and bleeds easily. On dark skin the swelling and shine of the nail fold are easier to see than redness.

The big toe is affected in most cases, on one or both sides. Teenagers and young adults are the usual patients, along with older people whose nails have thickened.

## How to spot it

- Pressing the side of the toe, not the tip, causes sharp pain.
- The corner of the nail cannot be seen because skin covers it.
- Shoes make it worse; sandals ease it.
- There is no injury to explain a sudden swollen toe.

A swollen nail fold at the base of the nail, often on a finger, is [paronychia](/conditions/paronychia/).

## Causes and triggers

A nail trimmed too short leaves a sharp spike at the corner that pierces the skin as the nail grows forward. Pressure from narrow shoes pushes the skin into the nail. Sweat softens the skin, and closed shoes in a hot climate make this worse. Some people simply have strongly curved nails.

> [!DERM] See a doctor if
> - there is pus, spreading redness or warmth
> - pain has lasted more than two weeks
> - the problem keeps coming back
> - you have diabetes, poor circulation or numb feet; do not treat it yourself

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - "Bathroom surgery": digging out the corner with a blade or clippers
> - Cutting a V-shaped notch in the nail; it does not work
> - Tight shoes while the toe is sore
> - Repeated courses of antibiotics without treating the nail

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The doctor examines the toe and both feet, checks circulation and sensation, and tests a nail clipping if the nail is thick ([fungal nail infection](/conditions/fungal-nail-infection/)). People with diabetes have a wider foot check.

## Ingrown toenail treatment at the clinic

- **Conservative care** for early cases: soaks, a small roll of cotton or a splint under the nail corner to lift it, and footwear advice.
- **Partial nail avulsion with matrix treatment** for repeated, infected or overgrown cases. Only the ingrowing strip is removed, so the nail looks slightly narrower but otherwise normal. It rarely recurs after this procedure.
- **Dressings and review** until healed.

The procedure is part of [nail care](/services/nail-care/) and [foot skin care](/services/foot-skin-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Cut toenails straight across, level with the tip of the toe.
- Choose shoes with a wide toe box; check children's shoe size often.
- Keep feet dry and change socks daily.
- People with diabetes should have nails cut by a trained professional.
