## What rosacea looks like on brown and Black skin

Redness, the hallmark in light skin, appears on dark skin as a dusky, violet-brown or simply darker area across the cheeks and nose, sometimes noticed only by comparing with the neck. What the person feels is often clearer than what others see: heat, tightness and stinging, especially after sun, a hot meal or a new product.

Small, firm bumps and pus spots appear on the cheeks, nose, chin and central forehead and heal with dark marks. The skin is dry and reactive. Eyes may feel gritty. Thickening of the nose is uncommon.

Conditions that look similar include [acne](/conditions/acne/), [seborrhoeic dermatitis](/conditions/seborrhoeic-dermatitis-and-dandruff/), the butterfly rash of [lupus](/conditions/cutaneous-lupus/), and [steroid cream damage](/conditions/skin-damage-from-bleaching-and-steroid-creams/).

## How to spot it

- Your face flushes or feels hot several times a week.
- Many skincare products sting.
- Bumps are on the central face only, with no blackheads.
- Sun, heat or pepper make it worse within hours.

## Causes and triggers

The cause is not fully known. Overactive facial blood vessels, an exaggerated immune response in the skin, and a skin mite (Demodex) all play a part. Lagos sun and heat, pepper-rich meals and hot kitchens are everyday triggers.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - your face burns, flushes or has had bumps for more than a few weeks
> - acne treatment is making your face worse
> - your eyes are sore, gritty or sensitive to light
> - you have used a steroid or lightening cream on your face

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Steroid and "triple action" creams on the face
> - Scrubs, toners with alcohol, and strong acids
> - Fragranced products and menthol
> - Hot water on the face

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

Diagnosis is clinical: the pattern, your symptoms and your triggers. Dermoscopy shows fine vessels not visible to the eye. A blood test or [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) is arranged if lupus is a possibility.

## Rosacea treatment at the clinic

- **Skincare reset:** a soap-free cleanser, a plain moisturiser and a mineral or tinted broad-spectrum sunscreen.
- **Prescription creams or gels** for bumps and inflammation, used for at least eight to twelve weeks.
- **Tablets** for moderate to severe bumps or eye symptoms.
- **[Vascular laser](/services/laser-treatments/laser-vascular-treatment/)** for fixed redness and visible vessels, with settings chosen for dark skin after a test area.
- **Maintenance** treatment to prevent relapse.

Reviews can be held by [online consultation](/services/dermatology-consultations/online-consultation/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Use sunscreen every morning and seek shade at midday.
- Keep a trigger diary and adjust what you can.
- Introduce one new product at a time, testing near the jaw first.
- Let hot drinks and food cool slightly.
