## What it looks like on brown and Black skin

Darkening in body folds takes two main forms, and telling them apart decides the treatment.

**Friction and irritation darkening.** Flat brown to dark brown patches with normal skin texture, in the underarms, bikini line and inner thighs, often with ingrown hairs, bumps or dry, irritated skin. The thighs are darkest where they rub together.

**Acanthosis nigricans.** The skin is darker and also thicker, with a soft, velvety or ridged surface that can look like dirt that will not wash off. It appears at the back and sides of the neck, in the armpits and groin, under the breasts, and sometimes on the knuckles, elbows and face. [Skin tags](/conditions/skin-tags/) often grow in the same areas.

Other patterns:

- **Dark knuckles, elbows, knees and ankles** from pressure and friction, which stand out more after lightening creams have made the surrounding skin paler. See [skin damage from bleaching and steroid creams](/conditions/skin-damage-from-bleaching-and-steroid-creams/).
- **A dark, itchy, scaly groin rash with a sharp edge,** which is fungal; see [ringworm](/conditions/ringworm-and-fungal-skin-infections/).
- **Dark, slightly scaly underarm patches** from a bacterial condition called erythrasma.

## How to spot it

- Is the skin only darker, or darker and thicker? Thick and velvety points to acanthosis nigricans.
- Does it itch or sting after shaving or deodorant? That points to irritation.
- Is there a rash with an edge, scale or smell? That points to infection.
- Are you gaining weight around the middle, or do you have irregular periods or a family history of diabetes?

## Causes and triggers

- **Friction:** thighs rubbing, tight underwear, bra straps, leaning on elbows, kneeling, scrubbing with sponges.
- **Hair removal:** shaving, waxing, plucking and depilatory creams.
- **Deodorants and perfumes,** especially sprays and fragranced products applied to just-shaved skin.
- **Raised insulin:** excess weight, prediabetes, [type 2 diabetes](/services/internal-medicine/diabetes/) and polycystic ovary syndrome. Insulin acts on skin cells and makes them multiply.
- **Medicines:** some hormonal treatments, steroid tablets and high-dose niacin.
- **Lightening products,** which cause rebound darkening, thinning and stretch marks in folds.
- **Rarely,** acanthosis nigricans that appears suddenly and spreads quickly in an adult who is losing weight can signal an internal illness and needs prompt medical assessment.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - the skin is thick and velvety as well as dark
> - darkening is spreading or appeared quickly
> - there is itching, scaling, soreness or smell
> - you have used lightening creams in these areas
> - a child or teenager has a dark, velvety neck

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Bleaching creams, soaps and "underarm whitening" products, which thin fold skin and cause stretch marks
> - Lemon, lime, baking soda and toothpaste
> - Scrubbing; friction is one of the causes
> - Mixed steroid creams
> - Deodorant on freshly shaved or broken skin

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The dermatologist examines the texture and pattern of the skin. A Wood's lamp or a [skin scraping](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/fungal-tests/) is used if infection is possible. Where acanthosis nigricans is present, your weight, waist and blood pressure are measured and blood tests for glucose and, where relevant, hormones are arranged, together with the clinic's [internal medicine service](/services/internal-medicine/general-medical-consultation/). Read [Diabetes and your skin: signs to watch](/blog/diabetes-and-your-skin/).

## Dark inner thighs and underarms treatment at the clinic

**1. Treat the cause.**

- *Insulin resistance:* weight loss, activity, dietary change and medical treatment where needed. The skin improves as insulin falls.
- *Friction:* looser cotton clothing, anti-chafing shorts, moisturiser to reduce rubbing.
- *Hair removal:* switch to trimming, or to [hair removal by laser](/services/laser-treatments/laser-hair-removal/) with a laser suited to dark skin.
- *Irritation:* fragrance-free deodorant; none on broken skin.
- *Infection:* antifungal or antibacterial treatment.

**2. Improve the skin.**

- Moisturisers with urea or lactic acid to soften thick skin
- A prescription retinoid to thin and smooth velvety areas
- Pigment-suppressing creams for a limited period under supervision
- A series of [superficial chemical peels](/services/aesthetic-treatments/chemical-peels/)

Skin in folds is thin and occluded, so it absorbs more and irritates easily. Strengths are kept low and reviewed.

**3. Be patient.** Expect gradual lightening over three to six months. Complete matching with the rest of the body is not always possible, since folds are naturally darker.

## Prevention and daily care

- Keep folds dry; pat, do not rub.
- Wear breathable cotton and avoid tight seams.
- Moisturise after bathing to cut friction.
- Space out shaving; use a sharp blade and shaving gel.
- Keep to a healthy weight and stay active.
- Have blood sugar checked if you have a dark, velvety neck or underarms.
