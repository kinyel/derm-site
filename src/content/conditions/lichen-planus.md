## What lichen planus looks like on brown and Black skin

The violet colour described in textbooks appears on dark skin as deep purple-black, slate-grey or dark brown. Bumps are a few millimetres across, flat on top, and shine when light falls across them; a magnifier shows fine white lines called Wickham's striae. They cluster on the inner wrists, forearms, ankles, shins and lower back, and may appear in lines along scratches. On the shins they often merge into thick, rough, very itchy plaques (hypertrophic lichen planus), which is common in African patients.

In the mouth, look for a lace-like white pattern on the inner cheeks, or sore red areas. On the scalp it causes patches of permanent hair loss ([scarring alopecia](/conditions/scarring-alopecia/)).

## How to spot it

- Bumps are flat-topped, not dome-shaped.
- The itch is severe.
- Wrists and ankles are involved.
- There are white streaks inside the mouth.

## Causes and triggers

The trigger is unknown in most people. Some cases are linked to hepatitis C, and some medicines, including certain blood pressure and anti-malarial medicines, cause a similar eruption.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - you have very itchy dark bumps that last more than two weeks
> - there is soreness in the mouth or on the genitals
> - nails are thinning or splitting
> - hair is being lost in patches with scalp tenderness

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Scratching, which spreads lesions
> - Lightening creams on the marks
> - Spicy and acidic foods and tobacco if the mouth is affected

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

A [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) confirms the diagnosis. Blood tests for hepatitis are arranged, and your medicines are reviewed.

## Lichen planus treatment at the clinic

Potent steroid ointment is the main treatment, with injections for thick plaques. Widespread disease may need [phototherapy](/services/clinical-dermatology/phototherapy/) or tablets. Mouth and genital disease are treated with specific preparations and followed up. When inflammation has settled, pigmentation is addressed as described under [hyperpigmentation](/conditions/hyperpigmentation/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Moisturise and keep nails short.
- Protect affected skin from sun.
- Attend review for mouth or genital disease.
