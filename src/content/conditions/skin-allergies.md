## What skin allergies look like on brown and Black skin

Redness is muted on dark skin, so allergic rashes appear as swelling, raised bumps, a darker, violet or greyish tone, and itch. Hives are smooth swellings the colour of the skin. Contact allergy is a rough, sometimes weeping patch that matches what was touched. A drug rash is usually many small bumps spreading from the trunk. A fixed drug eruption is one or a few sharply round, dusky patches that return to the same spot.

## How to spot it

- **Timing:** minutes to hours (hives), one to three days (contact), days to weeks (medicines).
- **Place:** where something touched, or all over.
- **Repetition:** the same trigger each time.

## Causes and triggers

Common contact allergens are hair dye, fragrance, nickel, preservatives, rubber and adhesives. Common drug causes include antibiotics, sulfa medicines, anti-seizure medicines and anti-inflammatory painkillers. Hives are often triggered by infection. See [hives](/conditions/hives/) and [contact dermatitis](/conditions/contact-dermatitis/) for detail.

> [!URGENT] Go to an emergency department if
> - the lips, tongue or throat swell, or breathing is difficult
> - a rash comes with fever, blisters, peeling skin, or sores in the mouth, eyes or genitals
> - you feel faint or collapse

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - a rash keeps returning and you cannot identify the cause
> - you need to know which products or medicines to avoid

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Re-trying a suspected medicine or product
> - Steroid injections or tablets taken repeatedly without a diagnosis
> - Cutting out many foods on the basis of unvalidated tests

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

History and examination identify the type. [Patch testing](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/patch-testing/) is performed at the clinic for contact allergy. A [skin biopsy](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/skin-biopsy/) can help confirm a drug rash. Referral to an allergy service is arranged for suspected food or sting allergy.

## Skin allergy treatment at the clinic

Avoidance is the cure. Meanwhile, antihistamines control hives, a suitable steroid cream settles contact dermatitis, and a suspected medicine is changed in consultation with the prescriber. You receive a written list of what to avoid and the names it appears under.

## Prevention and daily care

- Keep a record of every medicine or product that has caused a reaction and show it to each doctor and pharmacist.
- Choose fragrance-free products.
- Test new hair dye as the pack instructs.
