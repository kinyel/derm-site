## What burns look like on brown and Black skin

- **Superficial:** painful, dry, slightly swollen. The skin is darker and tender; redness is hard to see. It peels in a few days.
- **Partial thickness:** blisters, a wet, pink or pale base once the dark surface layer lifts, and marked pain. The loss of pigmented surface skin makes the wound look alarmingly light.
- **Full thickness:** white, brown, black or waxy, dry and firm, with reduced feeling.

Healed burns on dark skin commonly remain pale, or become darker, for many months. Deep burns can form thick raised scars that tighten across joints.

## How to judge severity

Use the person's palm (including fingers) as about 1% of body surface. Depth, size, site and age matter.

> [!URGENT] Go to an emergency department if
> - the burn is larger than the person's hand, or is deep (white, leathery or charred)
> - it is on the face, hands, feet, genitals or over a joint, or goes all the way round a limb
> - it was caused by chemicals, electricity or an explosion, or smoke was inhaled
> - the person is a baby, a young child, pregnant, elderly or has diabetes
> - there is fever, spreading swelling or pus

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Ice or iced water
> - Oils, butter, toothpaste, egg, honey from the kitchen, herbal pastes
> - Cotton wool or fluffy dressings
> - Pulling off clothing stuck to the burn

## Causes and triggers

In Lagos homes the frequent causes are hot water and soup scalds in children, hot oil, kerosene and gas accidents, generator and motorcycle exhaust burns on the leg, and chemical burns from relaxers, drain cleaners and unsupervised peels. Chemical burns are flushed with running water for at least 20 minutes.

## How it is assessed at the clinic

The clinician records the cause, time, first aid, size and depth, checks tetanus status, and decides whether the burn can be managed with dressings or needs a burns unit.

## Burn treatment at the clinic

Minor burns are cleaned and covered with a non-stick dressing, changed every few days, with pain relief. Signs of infection are treated. Once healed, the area is moisturised, massaged and protected from sun. Raised scars are treated early with silicone and steroid injections; established scars may be suitable for [scar revision](/services/skin-surgery/scar-revision/). Ongoing dressings are provided within [wound care](/services/wound-care/).

## Prevention and daily care

- Turn pot handles inward and keep children out of cooking areas.
- Test bath water with your elbow; add cold water first.
- Store fuel and chemicals locked away; refuel generators only when cool.
- Cover the motorcycle-side leg with long trousers.
