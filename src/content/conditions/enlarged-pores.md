## What enlarged pores look like on brown and Black skin

Pores show as small, dark-rimmed openings, most obvious across the nose and the cheeks beside it. People with brown and Black skin often have larger, more active oil glands, so shine and visible pores are common. Blocked pores appear as blackheads; picking them leaves dark marks, which make the texture look worse.

## How to spot the cause

- Shiny by noon: oil is the main factor.
- Dark plugs: blockage.
- Oval or slit-shaped pores on the cheeks in your forties and beyond: loss of support from sun and age.
- Pitted areas: these may be [acne scars](/conditions/acne-scars/), not pores.

## Causes and triggers

Genes, hormones and climate set oil output. Thick creams, oils, heavy make-up and sunscreen that is not removed block pores. Sun breaks down the collagen that holds pore walls tight.

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - you also have persistent pimples or blackheads
> - products are irritating or darkening your skin
> - you would like a procedure for texture

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Scrubs, pore vacuums and squeezing
> - Alcohol-based toners that strip the skin and trigger more oil
> - Heavy oils and butters on the face
> - Lightening creams

## How it is assessed at the clinic

The dermatologist examines the skin, distinguishes pores from scars and other conditions, and reviews your routine.

## Enlarged pores treatment at the clinic

A routine built on a salicylic acid product, a retinoid at night, niacinamide and a light sunscreen is the base. A series of superficial [chemical peels](/services/aesthetic-treatments/chemical-peels/) clears plugs; [microneedling](/services/aesthetic-treatments/microneedling/) or carefully chosen laser improves the support around pores. [Medical facials](/services/aesthetic-treatments/medical-facials/) can include professional extraction with less risk of marks.

## Prevention and daily care

- Cleanse twice a day; remove sunscreen and make-up at night.
- Use oil-free, non-comedogenic products.
- Wear sunscreen daily.
