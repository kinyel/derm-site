## What cold sores look like on brown and Black skin

A patch at the lip border tingles or burns, then swells slightly. Within a day, several tiny blisters filled with clear fluid appear close together on a base that is darker or dusky. They merge, burst and leave a shallow sore that forms a yellow-brown crust. As it heals, the area may stay darker for weeks, particularly if it was picked.

Sores can also appear around the nostrils, on the chin or cheek. In children, the first infection may cause many painful ulcers inside the mouth with fever and refusal to eat or drink.

## How to spot it

- It recurs in the same place.
- A tingle or itch comes first.
- Small grouped blisters appear, not a single pimple.
- It follows a fever, sun exposure or stress.

Cracks at the corners of the mouth are usually angular cheilitis, not herpes. Honey-coloured crusts that spread in a child suggest [impetigo](/conditions/impetigo/).

## Causes and triggers

After the first infection the virus travels along a nerve and rests in a nerve root for life. Triggers allow it to travel back to the skin. Strong sun during harmattan or at the beach, and the fevers common in Lagos, are frequent triggers.

> [!URGENT] Get urgent medical help if
> - a sore or blisters appear near or in the eye, or the eye is red and painful
> - a person with eczema develops widespread painful blisters
> - a baby under one month has any blister, or has been kissed by someone with a cold sore and becomes unwell
> - the person has weak immunity and sores are spreading

> [!DERM] See a dermatologist if
> - sores are frequent, large or slow to heal (over two weeks)
> - you are unsure whether it is a cold sore
> - you are planning a lip filler, peel or laser and have a history of cold sores

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Kissing babies, and anyone with eczema or weak immunity, while you have a sore
> - Picking or squeezing blisters
> - Touching your eyes after touching the sore
> - Steroid creams on the sore
> - Sharing lip balm, cups and towels during an attack

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

Cold sores are recognised on examination. A swab for the virus is taken when the appearance is unusual, the sore will not heal, or immunity is reduced.

## Cold sore treatment

- **Antiviral cream** applied five times a day from the first tingle shortens the attack slightly.
- **Antiviral tablets** work better, especially if started within a day of symptoms; a dermatologist can prescribe a supply to keep at home.
- **Comfort:** petroleum jelly to prevent cracking, a cool compress and simple pain relief.
- **Prevention** tablets for frequent or severe recurrences, and before facial procedures such as [dermal fillers](/services/aesthetic-treatments/dermal-fillers/) and [laser resurfacing](/services/laser-treatments/laser-skin-resurfacing/).

An [online consultation](/services/dermatology-consultations/online-consultation/) is suitable for arranging treatment early in an attack.

## Prevention and daily care

- Wear a lip balm with SPF outdoors.
- Wash hands after applying cream.
- Replace your toothbrush after an attack.
- Tell your clinician about cold sores before any lip or facial treatment.
