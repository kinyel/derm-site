## What cracked heels look like on brown and Black skin

The rim of the heel develops a collar of thickened skin that looks ashy, grey-white or yellow-brown against the darker foot. Lines appear in it, running up from the sole. As the pad of fat under the heel spreads sideways with each step, the lines open into splits. Dirt settles in the cracks and they look black.

Shallow cracks are a nuisance. Deep ones reach living skin, hurt, bleed and let bacteria in.

## How to spot it

- Cracks are at the edge of the heel, on both feet.
- They are worse in the dry season and with slippers.
- The rest of the sole is dry.

Check also for scaling between the toes or over the whole sole ([athlete's foot](/conditions/athletes-foot/)), well-defined thick scaly plaques ([psoriasis](/conditions/psoriasis/)), and a painful hard spot under a pressure point ([corns and calluses](/conditions/corns-and-calluses/)).

## Causes and triggers

Heel skin has no oil glands and depends on sweat for moisture. Harmattan air, air-conditioning, hot baths and soap dry it. Open-backed footwear lets the heel pad expand and the skin edge take the strain. Weight and long standing increase the load. Diabetes dries the feet and dulls the pain that would otherwise warn of a deep crack.

> [!DERM] See a doctor if
> - cracks are deep, bleeding or very painful
> - there is redness, swelling, warmth or pus
> - you have diabetes, numb feet or poor circulation
> - a month of daily care has not helped
> - the soles are scaly or itchy as well

> [!AVOID] What to avoid
> - Razor blades and metal scrapers
> - Soaking in very hot water, bleach or strong detergents
> - Walking barefoot on hard or hot ground
> - Superglue from the toolbox on deep cracks; medical skin adhesive is different and is applied by a clinician

## How it is diagnosed at the clinic

The dermatologist examines both feet, the nails and the hands. A scraping tests for fungus ([fungal tests](/services/skin-tests-and-diagnostics/fungal-tests/)), and blood sugar or thyroid tests are arranged where the history suggests them.

## Cracked heels treatment at the clinic

- **Paring** of thick skin with sterile instruments, painlessly.
- **Prescription-strength keratolytic balm** and an overnight occlusion routine.
- **Closure** of deep cracks with medical adhesive or strapping so they can heal.
- **Treatment of the cause,** such as fungal infection or psoriasis.
- **Footwear advice** and heel cups to hold the heel pad.

See [foot skin care](/services/foot-skin-care/) for the service.

## Prevention and daily care

- Moisturise the feet every night; more often in harmattan.
- Wear shoes or sandals with a closed, cushioned heel.
- File lightly once or twice a week.
- Inspect your heels weekly, daily if you have diabetes.
