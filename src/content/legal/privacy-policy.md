This policy explains what personal data {{clinic}} collects through this website, why, and what your rights are under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). It covers the website only. Records created when you attend the clinic are covered by the clinic's patient privacy notice, which you can ask for at reception.

## Who is responsible for your data

{{clinic}}, {{address}}, is the data controller. You can reach us on {{phone}} or through the [contact form](/contact/).

## What we collect and why

| Data | When | Why | Lawful basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, phone or WhatsApp number, preferred date and time, the service you are asking about, whether you are a new or returning patient, whether you want to be seen in person or online, and any message you write | When you send the booking or contact form | To reply to you and arrange your appointment | Your consent, and steps taken at your request before providing a service |
| Messages you send by WhatsApp, phone or email | When you contact us through those channels | To reply and arrange your appointment | Your consent and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries |
| Pages visited, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, and the site that referred you | Only if you accept analytics cookies | To understand which pages are useful and to improve the site | Your consent |

We ask you not to put detailed medical information in the form. If you do, we treat it as sensitive personal data and use it only to arrange your care.

## What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your details for marketing unless you have asked to hear from us. We do not make decisions about you by automated means.

## Who receives your data

Your form request is delivered to the clinic by email through service providers that process it on our behalf: Cloudflare (website hosting, form handling and bot protection) and an email delivery provider. If you accept analytics, Google (Google Analytics 4) and Microsoft (Clarity) process usage data. Pages that show the clinic's map load it from Google Maps, so Google receives your IP address when the map appears (see the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/)). Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria; where they do, we rely on the safeguards permitted by the NDPA.

## How long we keep it

Booking requests that do not lead to an appointment are deleted within 12 months. If you become a patient, your details form part of your clinical record and are kept for as long as the law and professional guidance require.

## Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to withdraw consent at any time, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, contact the clinic. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Cookies

See the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/). Analytics cookies are set only after you accept them, and you can change your choice at any time.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Form requests are transmitted encrypted, protected against automated abuse, and accessible only to staff who need them to arrange your appointment.

## Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the date of the latest update at the top.
