This page explains how the health information on this website is produced, so you can judge how far to rely on it.

## Who writes it

Pages are written by the {{clinic}} editorial team and carry that byline. When a doctor has reviewed a page, the page says so, with the reviewer's name and the date. A page without a review line has not yet been through that step.

## What we base it on

We write from established clinical guidance and peer-reviewed research, including:

- the UK National Health Service (NHS)
- the American Academy of Dermatology (AAD)
- the British Association of Dermatologists (BAD)
- the World Health Organization (WHO)
- DermNet, the dermatology reference resource
- peer-reviewed journals, with a preference for systematic reviews and guidelines

Each medical page lists its references. Where studies were done mainly in people with light skin, we say what is known about brown and Black skin and where the evidence is thin.

## How we write

- **Answer first.** The opening paragraph answers the main question directly.
- **Plain words.** We aim for language a 12-year-old can follow, and we explain medical terms when we use them.
- **Local reality.** We describe how conditions look on brown and Black skin and how Lagos heat, humidity and sun affect them.
- **No promises.** We describe what treatment is intended to do and its risks. We do not promise results, publish testimonials or show before-and-after galleries.
- **No doses, no do-it-yourself procedures.** The right medicine and dose depend on the person, so we do not give them.
- **Newer treatments.** Where evidence is still emerging, we say what is and is not known.

## Keeping pages current

Every medical page shows the date it was last updated. Pages are checked when guidance changes, and the date is changed only when the content has been changed.

## Independence

We do not accept payment to mention products, devices or companies. Where a product type is mentioned, it is because it is relevant to the condition.

## Corrections

If you think something on this site is wrong or out of date, please tell us through the [contact page](/contact/). We review reports and correct errors, and we update the page date when we do.

## Not a substitute for care

Information on this site is general. It cannot replace a consultation. See the [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer/).
