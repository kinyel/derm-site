Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. This page lists what this site uses.

## What the site stores without asking

| Name | Type | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `oc-consent` | Local storage | Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics, so we do not ask on every page | Until you clear your browser data |

The site works fully without any other storage.

## Analytics: only if you accept

If you choose "Accept analytics", two tools are loaded:

| Tool | Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics 4 | Google | Counts visits and shows which pages are read, with IP addresses shortened |
| Clarity | Microsoft | Shows, in aggregate, how pages are scrolled and where people tap, so we can fix confusing layouts |

Both set their own cookies. If you choose "Decline", neither tool is loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

## Security check on the form

The contact form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a submission comes from a person. It runs only when you use the form and may set a short-lived cookie for that purpose.

## Maps

The map on our home, contact and location pages comes from Google Maps. It loads by itself when you reach that part of the page, so your browser contacts Google at that point, and Google may set its own cookies and receive your IP address. If you would rather not load it, block third-party content in your browser; the address and the "Open in Google Maps" link still work.

## Changing your choice

<a href="#" data-consent-reset>Open cookie settings</a> to change your analytics choice. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.
