## General information only

The articles, condition guides and treatment pages on this website are written to help you understand skin, hair, nail and general health conditions. They describe how conditions usually behave and how they are usually treated. They cannot take account of your own health, medicines, allergies or skin type.

Nothing on this site is a diagnosis, a prescription or personal medical advice, and reading it does not make you a patient of {{clinic}}.

## Do not delay seeing a doctor

Do not ignore a symptom, delay seeking care or stop a prescribed medicine because of something you have read here. Many skin conditions look alike, and some serious conditions look minor at first. A doctor needs to examine you to tell them apart.

## Emergencies

This website is not monitored for emergencies. Go to the nearest hospital emergency department if you have:

- a rash with fever, blistering, peeling skin or sores in the mouth or eyes, especially after starting a new medicine
- swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, or difficulty breathing
- a rapidly spreading, hot, painful area of skin with fever
- shingles affecting the eye or the tip of the nose
- chest pain, weakness on one side, confusion, or very high blood pressure or blood sugar with symptoms

## Treatments and results

Descriptions of treatments explain what they are intended to do and the risks involved. Results vary from person to person, and no outcome is promised. Whether a treatment is suitable for you can be decided only at a consultation.

Some treatments described here are newer, and the evidence for them is still developing. Where that is the case, the page says so.

## Medicines and products

We do not give doses or name prescription-strength products on this site, because the right medicine and dose depend on the person. Never use a prescription cream, tablet or injection that was not prescribed for you.
